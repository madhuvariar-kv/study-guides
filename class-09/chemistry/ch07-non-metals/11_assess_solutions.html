Question 6 · Making chlorine in the lab

6. ക്ലോറിൻ വാതകം ലാബിൽ നിർമ്മിക്കുന്നതുമായി ബന്ധപ്പെട്ട് ചുവടെ നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്ന ചോദ്യങ്ങൾക്ക് ഉത്തരമെഴുതുക. Answer these questions on preparing chlorine in the lab.

  2. ക്ലോറിൻ വാതകം ജലത്തിലൂടെ കടത്തിവിട്ട് ശേഖരിക്കുന്നു. കാരണം എന്ത്? Chlorine is passed through water before it is collected. Why?
  3. ക്ലോറിൻ വാതകം ഗാഢ സൾഫ്യൂരിക് ആസിഡിലൂടെ കടത്തിവിടുന്നു. കാരണം എന്ത്? Chlorine is passed through concentrated sulphuric acid. Why?
🎬 Step by step – follow the gas through the wash bottles (virtual teacher demo)
⚠ Chlorine is a poisonous gas. This preparation is done only by a teacher, in a fume cupboard. Concentrated HCl and concentrated H₂SO₄ burn the skin. Never try it at home.
a) Potassium permanganate (KMnO₄) and concentrated hydrochloric acid (HCl) (gently heated):
2KMnO₄ + 16HCl → 2KCl + 2MnCl₂ + 8H₂O + 5Cl₂
b) To remove the hydrogen chloride (HCl) vapour that comes along with the chlorine. HCl dissolves readily in water.
c) To remove water vapour (to dry the chlorine). Concentrated sulphuric acid absorbs moisture.
The dry chlorine is collected by upward displacement of air because it is denser than air.

Question 7 · Why moisture for bleaching?

7. ക്ലോറിന്റെ ബ്ലീച്ചിങ്ങിന് ഈർപ്പം ആവശ്യമാണ്. കാരണം വ്യക്തമാക്കുക. Moisture is needed for bleaching by chlorine. Explain the reason.

🧺 Dry cloth vs moist cloth in chlorine
Chlorine bleaches by oxidation with nascent oxygen നവജാത ഓക്സിജൻ, and this oxygen forms only when chlorine reacts with water:
H₂O + Cl₂ → HCl + HOCl (hypochlorous acid ഹൈപ്പോക്ലോറസ് ആസിഡ്)
HOCl → HCl + [O]
The nascent oxygen [O] oxidises the coloured substance to a colourless one. Dry chlorine cannot form HOCl, so it cannot bleach: a dry cloth keeps its colour, a moist cloth loses it.

Question 8 · Give up chemical fertilisers completely?

8. “രാസവളം പൂർണമായി ഉപേക്ഷിച്ച് ജൈവവള പ്രയോഗം പ്രോത്സാഹിപ്പിക്കണം” എന്ന വാദഗതിയോട് നിങ്ങളുടെ അഭിപ്രായമെന്ത്? ഉത്തരം സാധൂകരിക്കുക. What is your opinion on the argument “Chemical fertilisers should be given up completely and the use of organic manure should be encouraged”? Justify your answer.

An opinion question has no single “right” side. Marks come from reasons on both sides (Table 7.4) and a clear conclusion that follows from them.

⚖️ Weigh the points – which side does each one support?
Organic manure ജൈവവളം adds humus and improves the soil.
Organic manure has only a small amount of nutrients; very large quantities are needed.
Organic manure helps earthworms and useful soil microbes.
Chemical fertilisers രാസവളം supply nutrients in known amounts and act quickly.
Too much chemical fertiliser can harm the soil and pollute water.
Stopping chemical fertilisers completely may lower crop yields.
Organic manure can be made from farm and kitchen waste.
Organic manure is bulky and releases nutrients slowly.

My opinion: I agree that organic manure should be encouraged, but I do not agree that chemical fertilisers should be given up completely.
Points for the slogan: organic manure adds humus, improves soil texture and water-holding, helps earthworms and soil microbes, recycles farm waste and causes little pollution. Too much chemical fertiliser can spoil the soil and pollute water bodies.
Points against it: organic manure has a low nutrient content and is bulky, so very large amounts are needed, and it acts slowly. Chemical fertilisers supply measured amounts of nutrients quickly; dropping them all at once could lower crop yields and food production.
Conclusion: both have merits and limitations (Table 7.4). The best path is scientific, balanced use: plenty of organic manure for soil health, plus chemical fertilisers only in the amount the crop needs (for example after a soil test). go further Agricultural scientists call this integrated nutrient management.
Other opinions can also earn full marks if they give reasons on both sides and a conclusion that follows from them.

13.

  1. കാർബണിന്റെ വൈദ്യുത ചാലകമായ രൂപാന്തരമേത്? Which allotrope of carbon conducts electricity?
  2. ഇതിനു കാരണം വ്യക്തമാക്കുക. Explain the reason.
  3. ഈ രൂപാന്തരത്തിന്റെ രണ്ട് ഉപയോഗങ്ങൾ എഴുതുക. Write two uses of this allotrope.
🔋 Connect a battery and a bulb to each crystal

A flat, simplified picture: real diamond is a 3-D network, and the bonds are drawn only to show how many each atom has.

In diamond വജ്രം each carbon atom uses all four of its outer electrons (valency 4) to form covalent bonds with four other carbon atoms. No free electrons are left to move through the crystal and carry current, so diamond does not conduct electricity.
a) Graphite ഗ്രാഫൈറ്റ്.
b) In graphite each carbon atom is bonded to only three other carbon atoms in flat hexagonal layers. The fourth electron of each atom is free and can move along the layers, so graphite conducts electricity വൈദ്യുതചാലകത.
c) Any two: electrodes in dry cells (it conducts electricity); pencil lead (soft, leaves a mark on paper); lubricant സ്നേഹകം (layers slide over each other). The question is about a conductor, so make sure one of your two uses is the dry-cell electrode.

c) The clear lime water turns milky go further (insoluble calcium carbonate forms: Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O).
⚠️ Four traps in these questions
  • Hydrogen: burning splinter → “pop”. Oxygen: glowing splinter → bursts into flame (Q5, Q9).
  • Dry chlorine does not bleach; it needs water to form HOCl and nascent oxygen (Q7).
  • Diamond does not conduct (no free electrons); graphite does (one free electron per carbon) (Q12, Q13).
  • Water removes HCl; concentrated H₂SO₄ removes water vapour – do not swap them (Q6).

Extended activity 1 · A note on the chemistry of chlorine bleaching

1. ക്ലോറിന്റെ ബ്ലീച്ചിങ് രസതന്ത്രത്തെക്കുറിച്ച് കുറിപ്പ് തയ്യാറാക്കി അവതരിപ്പിക്കുക. Prepare and present a note on the chemistry of bleaching by chlorine.

🎬 Step by step – from chlorine to a white cloth
The chemistry of bleaching by chlorine
Chlorine is a greenish-yellow gas with a pungent smell. It is used to bleach cotton, paper and pulp, and to make bleaching powder ബ്ലീച്ചിങ് പൗഡർ.
Observation. A dry coloured cloth kept in dry chlorine does not lose its colour. A moist coloured cloth in chlorine loses its colour. So moisture is needed.
Explanation. Chlorine reacts with water to form hydrochloric acid and hypochlorous acid ഹൈപ്പോക്ലോറസ് ആസിഡ്:
  H₂O + Cl₂ → HCl + HOCl
Hypochlorous acid is unstable and gives nascent oxygen നവജാത ഓക്സിജൻ [O], oxygen in the atomic state, which is very reactive:
  HOCl → HCl + [O]
The nascent oxygen oxidises the coloured substance into a colourless substance:
  coloured substance + [O] → colourless substance
So chlorine bleaches by oxidation, and only in the presence of water.
Bleaching powder. Dry chlorine passed over dry slaked lime gives bleaching powder, Ca(OCl)₂ (it smells of chlorine):
  2Ca(OH)₂ + 2Cl₂ → Ca(OCl)₂ + CaCl₂ + 2H₂O
Uses. Bleaching cloth and paper; disinfecting അണുനാശിനി drinking water and wells; making antiseptics and pesticides കീടനാശിനി.
Safety. Chlorine is poisonous: even a little irritates the nose, throat and lungs. Bleaching powder and bleach must be kept closed, away from children, and never mixed with acids or other cleaners.

🔑 The ideas that answered everything on this page

Hydrogen: Zn + dilute HCl; burning splinter → pop; highest calorific value (143 kJ/g) but burns explosively and is hard to store. Oxygen: from KMnO₄; glowing splinter bursts into flame. Carbon: diamond (4 bonds, no free electrons, non-conductor) vs graphite (3 bonds, free electrons, conductor); CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂, lime water turns milky. Nitrogen: plant growth, tyres. Chlorine: KMnO₄ + conc. HCl; water removes HCl, conc. H₂SO₄ removes moisture; bleaches only when moist (HOCl → HCl + [O]); chloride test with AgNO₃ then NH₄OH.
