Note on hydrogen. Hydrogen is the most abundant element in the universe and the main component of the Sun and the stars; there is very little free hydrogen in air. It has one proton and one electron (the commonest isotope, protium, has no neutron). It was discovered by Henry Cavendish in 1766, who called it "inflammable air" കത്തുന്ന വായു. It is a colourless, odourless gas made of diatomic molecules (H₂), less dense than air, and it does not dissolve in water.

4 · Carbon, diamond and graphite (pp 137–140)

പീരിയോഡിക് ടേബിൾ പരിശോധിച്ച് കാർബണിന്റെ സ്ഥാനം, അറ്റോമിക നമ്പർ, ഇലക്ട്രോൺ വിന്യാസം എന്നിവ കണ്ടെത്തി സയൻസ് ഡയറിയിൽ രേഖപ്പെടുത്തുക. Find the position, atomic number and electron configuration of carbon from the periodic table and record them in your science diary.

Atomic number 6; electron configuration 2, 4; group 14, period 2. It has 4 outer electrons, so its valency is 4.
Allotropes രൂപാന്തരങ്ങൾ: different forms of the same element with different physical properties; the phenomenon is allotropy രൂപാന്തരത്വം. Crystalline: diamond, graphite, fullerene, graphene. Amorphous: charcoal, coke, lamp black, coal (Fig 7.3).

1. Four. 2. Four. 3. No: all four outer electrons of every carbon are used in bonds, so diamond does not conduct electricity.

Graphite (Fig 7.5) ഗ്രാഫൈറ്റ്

  1. ഗ്രാഫൈറ്റിൽ ഓരോ കാർബൺ ആറ്റവും ചുറ്റുമുള്ള എത്ര കാർബൺ ആറ്റങ്ങളുമായി സഹസംയോജക ബന്ധനത്തിൽ ഏർപ്പെടുന്നു? In graphite, each carbon atom forms covalent bonds with how many carbon atoms?
  2. ഗ്രാഫൈറ്റിലെ ഓരോ കാർബൺ ആറ്റത്തിലെയും സ്വതന്ത്ര ഇലക്ട്രോണുകളുടെ എണ്ണം എത്ര? How many free electrons are there on each carbon atom in graphite?
  3. ഗ്രാഫൈറ്റ് വൈദ്യുതി കടത്തി വിടാനുള്ള കാരണം എന്ത്? Why does graphite conduct electricity?
1. Three. 2. One. 3. Each carbon uses only three of its four outer electrons in bonds; the fourth electron is free to move through the layers and carries the current. The layers are held together only by weak forces, so they slide over one another: graphite is a lubricant സ്നേഹകം.

Write the property that makes each possible: a. as an electrode in a dry cell; b. to make pencil "lead"; c. as a lubricant.

a. It conducts electricity (free electrons). b. It is soft and leaves a dark mark on paper (layers rub off). c. Its layers slide over one another (only weak forces between layers).

For organic manure: keeps soil healthy and full of microbes and earthworms; no acidity or pollution of wells, rivers and backwaters; cheap, made from farm waste (cow dung, compost, green leaves).
For chemical fertilisers: quick, measured supply of N, P and K; high yield to feed a large population; easy to store and transport (e.g. from FACT, Udyogamandal).

10 · Oxygen balance and the ozone layer (p 147)

  1. അന്തരീക്ഷ വായുവിൽ ഓക്സിജന്റെ അളവ് ഒരു പരിധിയിൽ കുറയാതെ സ്ഥിരമായി നിലനിർത്തുന്നതിന് സസ്യങ്ങൾക്കുള്ള പങ്കിനെക്കുറിച്ച് ഒരു കുറിപ്പ് തയ്യാറാക്കി ക്ലാസിൽ അവതരിപ്പിക്കുക. Prepare a note on the role of plants in keeping the amount of oxygen in the air steady, and present it in class.
  2. ഓസോൺ ശോഷണവും പരിഹാരമാർഗങ്ങളും എന്ന വിഷയത്തെ ആസ്പദമാക്കി ഒരു സെമിനാർ സംഘടിപ്പിക്കുക. Organise a seminar on ozone depletion and its remedies.
1. Plants and the oxygen balance. Living things use up oxygen in respiration, and burning fuels uses more. Green plants (and algae in the sea) make food by photosynthesis: they take in carbon dioxide and water and, in sunlight, give out oxygen. This returns oxygen to the air as fast as it is used, so its amount stays steady at about 21 %. Cutting forests and filling paddy fields and wetlands upsets this balance; planting trees and protecting mangroves and the Western Ghats forests helps keep it.
2. Seminar outline. (a) Ozone (O₃) in the stratosphere absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation അൾട്രാവയലറ്റ് വികിരണങ്ങൾ: 3O₂ ⇌ 2O₃ (UV). (b) Depletion: CFCs ക്ലോറോഫ്ലൂറോകാർബണുകൾ from old refrigerators, ACs and sprays rise to the stratosphere, break up and release chlorine, which destroys ozone. (c) Effects: more UV reaches the Earth – sunburn, skin cancer, eye cataracts, harm to crops and sea life. (d) Remedies: stop CFCs (Montreal Protocol); use substitutes such as HFCs, liquid hydrocarbons, ammonia and solid CO₂; dispose of old fridges and ACs properly; spread awareness on International Ozone Day, 16 September. Seminar support is on page 8.
