The coating forms by this reaction:
Fe + CuSO4 → FeSO4 + Cu   (CuSO4 contains Cu2+ and SO42− ions; FeSO4 contains Fe2+ and SO42− ions.)
(a) Using the atomic masses Fe = 56, Cu = 64, S = 32, O = 16, find the total mass of the reactants and of the products (in u). Which law does this show?
(b) Find the oxidation number of sulphur in CuSO4 and in FeSO4.
(c) Write the oxidation numbers of iron and copper before and after the reaction. Which is oxidised and which is reduced?
(d) Name the oxidising agent and the reducing agent. Is this a redox reaction? Why?

• The water drawn from a nearby well was muddy. When it was left to stand, the mud settled; when it was filtered, mud was left on the filter paper.
• The filtered water looked clear, but when a torch beam was passed through a glass of it in a dark room, the path of the light could still be seen faintly.
• ORS solution made from packets was clear, and the path of the torch beam could not be seen in it.
• Kanji vellam (rice water) was served to the children.
(a) Is the muddy well water a true solution, a colloid or a suspension? Give two reasons.
(b) What does the faint path of light in the filtered water show? Name this effect and give the particle size of such particles.
(c) Is ORS solution a true solution, a colloid or a suspension? Write two properties of its particles.
(d) Kanji vellam is a colloid. Name its type, its dispersed phase and its dispersion medium.
