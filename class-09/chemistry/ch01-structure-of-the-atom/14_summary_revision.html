1. The chapter in one picture

🗺️ Structure of the atom – concept map

Read it clockwise from the top left: discharge-tube experiments revealed the electron and the proton; the atom models improved from Thomson to Rutherford to Bohr; the nucleus holds the protons and neutrons (Z and A); the electrons fill the shells by 2n²; atoms of one element can differ in neutrons (isotopes); and radioactive isotopes are useful in medicine, industry and dating.

2. The key boxes of the textbook

1Molecule (p. 8)തന്മാത്ര

A molecule is the smallest particle of a substance that has all the properties of the substance and can exist independently. – page 4

2Rutherford’s model (p. 12)സൗരയൂഥ മാതൃക · ന്യൂക്ലിയസ്

The atom has a central part, the nucleus, which is very small compared with the atom. All the positive charge and almost all the mass are in the nucleus. Electrons revolve very fast round the nucleus in circular orbits (solar system model). – page 6

3Bohr’s model (p. 13)ബോർ ആറ്റം മാതൃക · ഊർജനിലകൾ
  1. Electrons revolve in definite orbits.
  2. Each orbit has a definite energy (energy levels).
  3. In an orbit the electron’s energy does not change (stationary energy levels).
  4. The energy of the orbits increases with distance from the nucleus.
  5. Higher → lower orbit: energy given out; lower → higher: energy absorbed.
  6. Orbits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 … = shells K, L, M, N, O.

– page 7

4Atomic mass unit (p. 13)യൂണിഫൈഡ് അറ്റോമിക് മാസ് യൂണിറ്റ് (u)

The unified atomic mass unit (u) is the unit used to express the masses of atoms. Proton ≈ 1 u, neutron ≈ 1 u, electron ≈ 0. – page 7

5Z and A (p. 14)അറ്റോമിക നമ്പർ (Z) · മാസ് നമ്പർ (A)
Z = protons = electrons
A = protons + neutrons
neutrons = A − Z

– page 8

6Isotopes (p. 19)ഐസോടോപ്പുകൾ

Different atoms of the same element with the same atomic number and different mass numbers: 11H, 21H, 31H. – page 10

7Isobars (p. 20)ഐസോബാറുകൾ

Atoms with the same mass number and different atomic numbers: 4018Ar, 4019K, 4020Ca. – page 10

BoxCathode rays (p. 9)കാഥോഡ് രശ്മികൾ

Geissler (1854) made discharge tubes and suction pumps; Plücker saw a glow opposite the cathode move in a magnetic field; Hittorf (1869) and Goldstein (1876) showed that rays from the cathode cause it. – page 4

BoxMass of the electron (p. 10)e/m അനുപാതം · ഓയിൽ ഡ്രോപ്പ് പരീക്ഷണം

Thomson: e/m = 1.76 × 1011 C/kg. Millikan’s oil drop: charge 1.6 × 10−19 C. So mass = 9.1 × 10−31 kg. – page 4

BoxRadioactivity (p. 11)റേഡിയോ ആക്ടീവത

Uranium, thorium … give out radiations by themselves (Becquerel, 1896): α – positive, with mass; β – negative; γ – no charge, no mass. – page 5

BoxFundamental particles (p. 14)മൗലിക കണങ്ങൾ · ക്വാർക്കുകൾ

The electron cannot be divided further – it is a fundamental particle. Protons and neutrons are each made of 3 quarks, so they are not. – page 7

BoxIsotones (p. 20)ഐസോടോണുകൾ

Atoms with the same number of neutrons: 157N and 146C both have 8 neutrons. – page 10

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Mixing up Z and A, or writing them on the wrong side. In 2311Na the mass number 23 is on top and the atomic number 11 below, both on the left.
  • Neutrons = A + Z. Wrong – neutrons = A − Z.
  • Putting electrons in the nucleus. The nucleus has only protons and neutrons; electrons are in the shells outside it.
  • Saying cathode rays are positive, canal rays negative, or that canal rays come from the cathode. Cathode rays are negative and start at the cathode; canal rays are positive and come from the anode side, through the holes of the cathode.
  • “Isotopes are different elements.” Isotopes are atoms of the same element; isobars are atoms of different elements.
  • Putting 8 electrons in K, filling M before L is full, or more than 8 in the outermost shell. K holds 2, L holds 8 – and potassium is 2, 8, 8, 1, not 2, 8, 9.
  • Writing the mass of the electron as 1 u. It is 0.000548 u – taken as 0 in practice.
  • Saying electrons in Bohr’s model lose energy while revolving. That was the problem with Rutherford’s model; in Bohr’s model the energy stays constant in an orbit.
  • Thinking the atom is a solid ball. The gold foil experiment showed it is mostly empty space.
  • Mixing up the scientists. Thomson – electron, plum pudding; Goldstein – canal rays; Rutherford – nucleus, proton, solar system model; Chadwick – neutron; Bohr – energy levels.
