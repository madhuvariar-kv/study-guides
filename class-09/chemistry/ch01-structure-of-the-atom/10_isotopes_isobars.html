Isotopes, Isobars and Isotones ഐസോടോപ്പുകൾ · ഐസോബാറുകൾ

Can two atoms of the same element have different masses? Can atoms of different elements have the same mass number? Yes to both! Here you meet the three hydrogen atoms protium, deuterium and tritium, the carbon atoms used to date fossils, the radioactive isotopes used in hospitals and power stations, and the "same-mass" atoms argon, potassium and calcium. Then sort atom pairs yourself.

📖 What you need (pages 8–9 of this guide)
  • Atomic number Z = protons = electrons; mass number A = protons + neutrons; neutrons = A − Z (page 8).
  • Symbol notation 3517Cl: A on top, Z below. Orbit diagrams: nucleus "Np / Nn", electrons on rings (page 9).

2. Atomic number 1 is hydrogen.
3. They differ in the number of neutrons (0, 1, 2).
4. No – the mass numbers are 1, 2 and 3.
5. Protium 11H has no neutron.
⭐ Key idea – isotopes (textbook page 19)

ഒരേ അറ്റോമിക നമ്പറും വ്യത്യസ്ത മാസ് നമ്പറുമുള്ള ഒരേ മൂലകത്തിന്റെ വ്യത്യസ്ത ആറ്റങ്ങളാണ് ഐസോടോപ്പുകൾ.

Isotopes ഐസോടോപ്പുകൾ are different atoms of the same element having the same atomic number and different mass numbers. (Same protons, different neutrons.) Protium പ്രോട്ടിയം, deuterium ഡ്യൂറ്റീരിയം and tritium ട്രിഷിയം are the isotopes of hydrogen.

3. Properties of isotopes and heavy water

ഐസോടോപ്പുകൾ ഒരേ രാസസ്വഭാവം കാണിക്കുന്നു. എന്നാൽ ഭൗതിക സ്വഭാവങ്ങളിൽ ചെറിയ വ്യത്യാസങ്ങൾ കാണിക്കുന്നു. – Isotopes show the same chemical properties രാസസ്വഭാവം (they have the same number of electrons, arranged the same way), but small differences in physical properties ഭൗതിക സ്വഭാവങ്ങൾ such as mass and density (their nuclei have different masses).

💧 Heavy water ഘനജലം

ഘനജലം (Heavy water) ഹൈഡ്രജന്റെ ഐസോടോപ്പായ ഡ്യൂറ്റീരിയത്തിന്റെ ഓക്സൈഡാണ്. ഘനജലം ആണവ നിലയങ്ങളിൽ ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നു. Heavy water is the oxide of deuterium, D₂O (each molecule has two ²H atoms instead of two ¹H atoms). It is used in nuclear power stations ആണവ നിലയങ്ങൾ.

Go further: it looks like ordinary water and takes part in the same chemical reactions, but it is about 11 % denser (≈ 1.11 g/cm³), freezes at about 3.8 °C and boils at about 101.4 °C – a small difference in physical properties. India's pressurised heavy-water reactors, for example at Kaiga in Karnataka, use it to slow down neutrons.

🖼 Fig 1.11 – three carbon atoms

¹²C, ¹³C, ¹⁴C എന്നിവ കാർബണിന്റെ പ്രകൃതിദത്ത ഐസോടോപ്പുകളാണ്. 126C, 136C and 146C are the natural isotopes of carbon.

  • ¹²C is the most stable and the most abundant carbon isotope.
  • ¹³C is only about 1.1 % of all carbon. It is used to study the life processes in plants and animals.
  • ¹⁴C is a radioactive isotope റേഡിയോ ആക്ടീവ് ഐസോടോപ്പ്. It is used to find the age of fossils ഫോസിലുകളുടെ കാലപ്പഴക്കം.
  • Only the isotopes of hydrogen have special names. Others are named by mass number: carbon-12, carbon-13, carbon-14.
📊 Virtual lab: how much of each carbon isotope?

Values are approximate. ¹⁴C is so rare (about 1 atom in a million million, 10¹²) that you would almost never find one in a sample of 1000 – yet it is enough for dating.

🏺 Kerala connection – dating the past

When a plant or animal dies it stops taking in carbon, and the ¹⁴C in it slowly decays (go further: half of it in about 5730 years). By measuring how much ¹⁴C is left in bones, wood or charcoal, scientists find their age. Carbon dating is used for fossils and at archaeological sites like the Pattanam excavations near Kodungallur.

1. All three have the same mass number, 40 (18 + 22 = 19 + 21 = 20 + 20 = 40).
2. No – the atomic numbers are 18, 19 and 20, so they are three different elements.
⭐ Key idea – isobars (textbook page 20)

ഒരേ മാസ് നമ്പറും വ്യത്യസ്ത അറ്റോമിക നമ്പറുമുള്ള ആറ്റങ്ങളാണ് ഐസോബാറുകൾ.

Isobars ഐസോബാറുകൾ are atoms having the same mass number and different atomic numbers. They are atoms of different elements with the same total number of particles in the nucleus (protons + neutrons).

📘 Side box – isotones ഐസോടോണുകൾ

ന്യൂട്രോണുകളുടെ എണ്ണം തുല്യമായ ആറ്റങ്ങൾ ഐസോടോണുകൾ എന്നറിയപ്പെടുന്നു. Atoms with the same number of neutrons are called isotones. Example: 157N (15 − 7 = 8 neutrons) and 146C (14 − 6 = 8 neutrons).

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • "Isotopes are different elements" ✗ – isotopes are atoms of the same element; isobars are different elements.
  • "Isotopes have different chemical properties" ✗ – their chemical properties are the same; only physical properties differ slightly.
  • Writing deuterium as 22H ✗ – hydrogen always has Z = 1: 21H.

🧭 Summary
  • Isotopes: same element, same Z, different A (different neutrons) – ¹H, ²H, ³H; ¹²C, ¹³C, ¹⁴C. Same chemical properties, slightly different physical properties.
  • Heavy water = D₂O, used in nuclear power stations. ¹³C ≈ 1.1 % of carbon (life processes); ¹⁴C is radioactive (age of fossils).
  • Uses: I-131 thyroid, U-235 fuel, Co-60 cancer treatment, Na-24 pipeline leaks, Fe-59 anaemia.
  • Isobars: same A, different Z – ⁴⁰Ar, ⁴⁰K, ⁴⁰Ca. Isotones: same number of neutrons – ¹⁵N and ¹⁴C.
