2. The key boxes of the textbook

1Combination reaction (p. 98)സംയോജന രാസപ്രവർത്തനം

Two or more simple substances (elements or compounds) combine to form a new compound: N2 + 3H2 → 2NH3. – page 4

2Decomposition reaction (p. 99)വിഘടന രാസപ്രവർത്തനം

A compound breaks down to form two or more substances: CaCO3 → CaO + CO2. – page 4

3Double decomposition (p. 100)ദ്വിവിഘടന രാസപ്രവർത്തനം

Two compounds react by exchanging ions to form two new compounds: NaCl + AgNO3 → AgCl↓ + NaNO3. – page 5

4Displacement reaction (p. 101)ആദേശ രാസപ്രവർത്തനം

One element in a compound is replaced by another element: Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl2 + H2↑. – page 5

5Catalyst (p. 108)ഉൽപ്രേരകം · ഉൽപ്രേരണം

Substances that increase the rate of a reaction without themselves undergoing a permanent chemical change are catalysts: MnO2 in 2H2O2 → 2H2O + O2. – page 8

BoxAir bags (p. 99)വിഘടന രാസപ്രവർത്തനം

In a crash, sodium azide decomposes in about 0.03 s: 2NaN3 → 2Na + 3N2↑ – the nitrogen fills the bag. – page 4

BoxThree kinds of double decomposition (p. 100)അവക്ഷിപ്തരൂപീകരണ · വാതകരൂപീകരണ · നിർവീരീകരണം

Those giving a precipitate, those giving a gas, and those giving a compound that does not split into ions, such as water in neutralisation. – page 5

BoxThermite (p. 101)തെർമൈറ്റ്

Fe2O3 + 2Al → 2Fe + Al2O3 – so much heat that the iron formed is molten; used to weld rails. – page 5

BoxCollision theory (p. 103)കൊളിഷൻ സിദ്ധാന്തം · ഫലവത്തായ കൂട്ടിമുട്ടലുകൾ

Reactions happen when reactant particles collide; only collisions with enough energy give products – effective collisions. – page 6

BoxAllotropes of phosphorus (p. 104)രൂപാന്തരങ്ങൾ

White phosphorus catches fire in air at room temperature; red phosphorus does not – the nature of the reactant decides the rate. – page 7

BoxPhysical state (p. 106)ഭൗതികാവസ്ഥ

Solid ZnSO4 and Ba(NO3)2 do not react; their solutions give white BaSO4 at once. Petrol vapour explodes. – page 7

BoxTemperature in daily life (p. 108)താപനില

Batter ferments faster in warm weather than in the fridge; light sticks glow brighter when warm. – page 8

BoxCatalysis in nature (p. 109)എൻസൈമുകൾ

Enzymes are biological catalysts: amylase in saliva changes starch into maltose. – page 8

BoxCatalyst and magic (p. 109)ഉൽപ്രേരകം

A sugar cube burns only with a little ash on it (metal compounds in ash act as a catalyst). The bombardier beetle sprays a hot liquid made by enzyme-catalysed decomposition of hydrogen peroxide. – page 8

3. Facts & rules

📐 Facts & rules card

chemical change → new substances (colour · gas · smell · precipitate · heat or light)
factor → more collisions, or more particles with ≥ threshold energy → more effective collisions → faster
catalyst → faster, and left unchanged at the end

Physical and chemical changes ഭൗതികമാറ്റം · രാസമാറ്റം

In a chemical change new substances form, usually with an energy change. Signs: colour change (നിറവ്യത്യാസം) – sugar turns black on heating; a gas (വാതകം) – Na2CO3 + HCl fizzes; a precipitate (അവക്ഷിപ്തം) – lead acetate + KI gives yellow lead iodide (teacher demonstration only – lead compounds are poisonous); a smell (ഗന്ധം) – iron sulphide + HCl gives rotten-egg H2S (teacher only, in a fume cupboard – H2S is poisonous); heat – quicklime + water (exothermic, താപമോചക പ്രവർത്തനം). Melting, boiling and dissolving are physical changes.

The four types of reactions രാസപ്രവർത്തനം

TypePatternWhat happensExamples
Combination സംയോജന രാസപ്രവർത്തനംA + B → ABtwo or more substances join to give one productCaO + H2O → Ca(OH)2; N2 + 3H2 → 2NH3; 2SO2 + O2 → 2SO3
Decomposition വിഘടന രാസപ്രവർത്തനംAB → A + Bone compound breaks into two or more products (by heat: thermal decomposition, താപീയ വിഘടനം)CaCO3 → CaO + CO2; CuCO3 → CuO + CO2; 2NaN3 → 2Na + 3N2
Double decomposition ദ്വിവിഘടന രാസപ്രവർത്തനംAB + CD → AD + CBtwo compounds exchange ions; a precipitate, a gas or water formsNaCl + AgNO3 → AgCl↓ + NaNO3; CaCO3 + 2HCl → CaCl2 + H2O + CO2↑; HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H2O
Displacement ആദേശ രാസപ്രവർത്തനംA + BC → AC + Ban element replaces another element in a compoundZn + 2HCl → ZnCl2 + H2↑; Fe + CuSO4 → FeSO4 + Cu; Fe2O3 + 2Al → 2Fe + Al2O3

Quick test: count the reactants and products and look for a free element. One product → combination; one reactant → decomposition; an element on each side → displacement; two compounds → two (or more) compounds → double decomposition.

Rate of reaction and collision theory രാസപ്രവർത്തനവേഗം · കൊളിഷൻ സിദ്ധാന്തം

Reactions go at very different speeds: an explosion (സ്ഫോടനം) is over in a moment, a dental filling sets in minutes, rusting (തുരുമ്പിക്കൽ) takes days to months, weathering of rocks takes years. We slow down food spoiling with salting, preservatives (പ്രിസർവേറ്റീവുകൾ), refrigeration, drying, vacuum packing and pickling.

Collision theoryparticles must collide; only collisions with at least the threshold energy (ത്രെഷോൾഡ് എനർജി) are effective and give products
Threshold energythe minimum kinetic energy (ഗതികോർജം) reactant particles must have to change into products – heating does not change it; it gives more particles enough energy

The five factors രാസപ്രവർത്തനവേഗം

FactorExample from the bookReason (collision theory)
1. Nature of reactants അഭികാരകങ്ങളുടെ സ്വഭാവംdilute HCl: Mg fast, Zn slow, Cu no reaction; Na reacts violently with water, Mg very slowly; white P catches fire in air, red P does notsome substances give or take electrons, or break their bonds, much more easily
2. Concentration ഗാഢതMg ribbon fizzes faster in concentrated HCl than in dilute (ഗാഢ · നേർപ്പിച്ച); steel wool burns with sparks in pure oxygenmore particles per unit volume (യൂണിറ്റ് വ്യാപ്തം) → more collisions → more effective collisions
3. Surface area പ്രതലപരപ്പളവ്marble powder faster than a lump; eggshell powder; split firewood; magnesium powder in fireworks; solutions react faster than solids (ഭൗതികാവസ്ഥ)more particles exposed → more collisions at the same time
4. Temperature താപനിലNH4Cl + NaOH: ammonia smell stronger on warming; thiosulphate + HCl turns cloudy sooner when warm; batter ferments faster in warm weathermore kinetic energy → more particles reach the threshold energy → more effective collisions
5. Catalyst ഉൽപ്രേരകംMnO2 makes H2O2 give oxygen fast (the glowing incense stick bursts into flame); MnO2 is filtered off unchangedit increases the rate and is left unchanged (it provides an easier path – go further)

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Calling dissolving sugar or boiling water a chemical change. No new substance forms – these are physical changes.
  • Mixing up decomposition and double decomposition. Decomposition has one reactant; in double decomposition two compounds exchange ions.
  • Calling Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl2 + H2 a combination reaction. Zinc replaces hydrogen – it is displacement.
  • Thinking a catalyst is used up or changes the products. It is recovered unchanged; it only changes the rate.
  • Saying every collision gives products. Only effective collisions, with at least the threshold energy, do.
  • Thinking a big lump reacts faster because there is “more” of it. The powder reacts faster – it has more surface area.
  • Confusing the amount of product with the rate. More reactant gives more product; a faster rate gives it sooner.
  • Saying a higher temperature lowers the threshold energy. It gives more particles enough energy; a catalyst is what provides an easier path.
