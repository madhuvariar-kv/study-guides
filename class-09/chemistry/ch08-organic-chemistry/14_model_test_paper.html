Meera lives in Kochi. Her family cooks on LPG; the main component of the gas in the cylinder is butane. Her uncle drives a CNG autorickshaw, and many city buses also run on CNG. Natural gas reaches Kochi as LNG by ship at the terminal at Puthuvype, and GAIL’s pipeline carries it to factories and to the CNG filling stations. Meera’s teacher says CNG is an eco-friendly fuel.
(a) Write the condensed formula and the molecular formula of butane. Show that it fits the general formula of its family.

(d) Why is CNG called an eco-friendly fuel?
