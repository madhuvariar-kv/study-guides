C Organic compounds & hydrocarbons ഓർഗാനിക് സംയുക്തങ്ങൾ

Organic
compounds of carbon – except CO, CO2, carbonates and bicarbonates. There are far more carbon compounds (about ten times more) than compounds of all the other elements together.
History
Berzelius named organic chemistry and believed in a vital force (ജീവശക്തി). Wöhler (1828) made urea from ammonium cyanate; Kolbe (1845) acetic acid; Berthelot (1856) methane – the vital force theory ended.
Carbon
Table 8.1: Z = 6; electron configuration 2, 4; valency 4. Carbon shares electrons: covalent bonds.
Key box
a compound of only carbon and hydrogen (ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകൾ). Bonds between carbons: single C–C (ഏകബന്ധനം), double C=C (ദ്വിബന്ധനം), triple C≡C (ത്രിബന്ധനം).
Formulae
structural formula (ഘടനാവാക്യം) – every bond drawn; condensed formula (കണ്ടൻസ്ഡ് ഫോർമുല) – CH3–CH2–CH3; molecular formula (തന്മാത്രാസൂത്രം) – C3H8.

🔑 The rule behind every structure

Every carbon atom has exactly four bonds (valency 4). A double bond counts as two, a triple bond as three. To find the family from a molecular formula, count the carbons (n) and compare the hydrogens: 2n + 2 → alkane, 2n → alkene, 2n − 2 → alkyne.

3. Word roots, suffixes and the name machine നാമകരണം · പദമൂലം · പ്രത്യയം

IUPAC (International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry, founded 1919) names a hydrocarbon from two things: the number of carbon atoms gives the word root, and the kind of bond between carbons gives the suffix – -ane (single), -ene (double), -yne (triple).

4. Homologous series ഹോമലോഗസ് ശ്രേണി

🔑 Key box (p. 161)
  • All members can be written with one general formula.
  • Neighbouring members differ by –CH2– (1 carbon and 2 hydrogens).
  • They have similar chemical properties.
  • Their physical properties change gradually (gas → liquid as the chain grows).

5. Ring compounds വലയസംയുക്തങ്ങൾ

Alicyclic ആലിസൈക്ലിക് സംയുക്തങ്ങൾlike alkanes and alkenes, but closed into a ring. Name: prefix cyclo + the IUPAC name (cyclobutane, cyclopentene …).
Aromatic അരോമാറ്റിക് ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകൾring compounds with a characteristic smell: benzene C6H6, naphthalene C10H8 (moth balls, two fused benzene rings).

Benzene box: Michael Faraday discovered benzene in 1825; August Kekulé proposed (1865) its ring of six carbons with alternating double bonds. Catenation (കാറ്റിനേഷൻ): the ability of atoms of one element to join each other – carbon has it most. Reasons for so many carbon compounds: catenation, valency 4, single/double/triple bonds, chains, branches and rings, bonds with H, O, N and halogens.

6. Fuels: petroleum, coal and natural gas ഫോസിൽ ഇന്ധനം

Petroleum (crude oil, പെട്രോളിയം · ക്രൂഡ് ഓയിൽ) is a mixture of hydrocarbons – a fossil fuel found deep between hard rocks, formed from the remains of living things over thousands of years. It is separated by fractional distillation (അംശികസ്വേദനം) in a fractionating tower (ഫ്രാക്ഷണേറ്റിങ് ടവർ): hottest at the bottom (about 350 °C), coolest at the top (about 50 °C).

Coal കൽക്കരി · കാർബണൈസേഷൻ

Carbonisation: plant remains turn into carbon at high temperature and pressure in the absence of air. Coal is used as a fuel; to make coke, coal tar and coal gas; as a source of aromatic compounds (benzene, naphthalene, phenol); for synthetic petrol; and for some fertilisers, medicines and perfumes.

Natural gas പ്രകൃതിവാതകം

Mainly methane. CNG (സി.എൻ.ജി) – compressed natural gas, filled at high pressure, for less-polluting vehicles. LNG (എൽ.എൻ.ജി) – liquefied natural gas, made liquid at very high pressure. It causes less carbon pollution – eco-friendly. GAIL supplies natural gas in Kerala.

7. Greenhouse gases and global warming ആഗോളതാപനം

The warm Earth gives out infrared rays (ഇൻഫ്രാറെഡ് രശ്മികൾ). Greenhouse gases (ഹരിതഗൃഹവാതകങ്ങൾ) – CO2, CH4, N2O, CFCs (also ozone and water vapour) – trap part of them: the greenhouse effect (ഹരിതഗൃഹപ്രഭാവം), which keeps the Earth warm. Extra greenhouse gases raise the average temperature of the Earth and its atmosphere: global warming. Nitrogen is not a greenhouse gas.

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Drawing a carbon with 3 or 5 bonds. Count every carbon in your drawing: always 4 (a double bond counts 2, a triple bond 3).
  • Mixing up the general formulas. Alkane 2n + 2, alkene 2n, alkyne 2n − 2 – check with ethane C2H6, ethene C2H4, ethyne C2H2.
  • Calling CO2 or CaCO3 organic compounds. CO, CO2, carbonates and bicarbonates are not counted as organic.
  • Naming propene “propane”. Check the bond: -ane single, -ene double, -yne triple.
  • Thinking homologues differ by “CH3”. The step is –CH2– (1 C and 2 H).
  • Forgetting “cyclo” for ring compounds, or thinking benzene and cyclohexane have the same formula (C6H6 vs C6H12).
  • Saying nitrogen is a greenhouse gas. It is not; CO2, CH4, N2O and CFCs are.
  • Thinking the greenhouse effect itself is bad. It keeps the Earth warm; the extra greenhouse gases cause global warming.
🛡️ Safety

Petrol, kerosene and LPG are flammable – never play with them. Naphthalene and benzene are toxic – keep moth balls away from small children.
