Alkenes and Alkynes – Unsaturated Hydrocarbons ആൽക്കീനുകൾ · ആൽക്കൈനുകൾ

Ripening bananas give off a tiny amount of ethene, and the hot blue flame that welders use to cut steel burns ethyne (acetylene). Both have carbon atoms joined by more than one bond. On this page you meet the two unsaturated families, fill Tables 8.3 and 8.4, find two more general formulae, and then sort ten hydrocarbons into their families.

📖 What you need (pages 3 and 4 of this guide)
  • A double bond ദ്വിബന്ധനം C=C is two shared pairs; a triple bond ത്രിബന്ധനം C≡C is three shared pairs (page 3).
  • Alkanes have only single bonds, are saturated and follow CnH2n+2; condensed formula, e.g. CH3–CH2–CH3 (page 4).

1. Unsaturated hydrocarbons അപൂരിത ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകൾ

⭐ Unsaturated hydrocarbons (page 157)
കാർബൺ ആറ്റങ്ങൾക്കിടയിൽ ദ്വിബന്ധനമോ ത്രിബന്ധനമോ ഉള്ള ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണിനെ പൊതുവായി അപൂരിത ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബൺ എന്ന് വിളിക്കുന്നു.

Hydrocarbons with a double bond or a triple bond between carbon atoms are generally called unsaturated hydrocarbons അപൂരിത ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകൾ.

Why "unsaturated"? A carbon in a double or triple bond spends two or three of its four valencies on the other carbon, so it holds fewer hydrogen atoms than it could. Ethane C2H6 is "full"; ethene C2H4 and ethyne C2H2 are not.

ഏതെങ്കിലും രണ്ട് കാർബൺ ആറ്റങ്ങൾക്കിടയിൽ ഒരു ദ്വിബന്ധനമെങ്കിലും ഉള്ള ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകളെ ആൽക്കീനുകൾ (alkenes) എന്നു വിളിക്കുന്നു.

Hydrocarbons with at least one double bond between any two carbon atoms are called alkenes ആൽക്കീനുകൾ.

c. ആൽക്കീനുകളുടെ പൊതുവാക്യം എഴുതുക. Write the general formula of alkenes.

a. The number of hydrogen atoms is twice the number of carbon atoms. b. 2n. c. General formula of alkenes: CnH2n.
Compared with the alkane of the same n, an alkene has 2 fewer H: the second bond of C=C uses one valency on each of the two carbons, so two H must go.

🔭 Go further

A molecular formula alone does not always decide the family. C5H10 fits an alkene (pentene) but also a ring compound with only single bonds (cyclopentane, page 8). For open-chain hydrocarbons with at most one multiple bond – the ones in this chapter's questions – the rule above works, and the textbook answer for C5H10 is "alkene". Also, the position of the double bond can change: CH2=CH–CH2–CH3 is but-1-ene and CH3–CH=CH–CH3 is but-2-ene (both C4H8).

🏠 Alkenes and alkynes around you
  • Ethene C2H4 – a natural plant hormone that ripens fruit; also the raw material of polythene (plastic bags and bottles).
  • Propene C3H6 – raw material for polypropylene (buckets, chairs, food boxes).
  • Ethyne C2H2 (acetylene) – burnt with oxygen in the oxy-acetylene torch (about 3000 °C) to weld and cut metals.
⚠️ Calcium carbide ("carbide") is sometimes used illegally to ripen mangoes: with moisture it gives off acetylene and can carry poisonous impurities such as arsenic and phosphorus compounds. It is banned for fruit ripening – wash fruit well and avoid fruits that look evenly ripe outside but are raw inside.
⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Mixing up the general formulas ✗ – alkane 2n + 2, alkene 2n, alkyne 2n − 2.
  • Giving C2 of propene 2 H (CH2=CH2–CH3) ✗ – that carbon would have 5 bonds. It is CH2=CH–CH3.
  • Writing ethyne as CH2≡CH2 ✗ – each carbon has only one bond left: CH≡CH.
  • "Alkenes have a double bond, so they have double the hydrogens" ✗ – a double bond means fewer hydrogens.

🧭 Summary
  • Unsaturated hydrocarbons അപൂരിത ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകൾ have a C=C double bond or a C≡C triple bond.
  • Alkenes ആൽക്കീനുകൾ: at least one C=C. Ethene C2H4, CH2=CH2 (Fig 8.5). Table 8.3: C2H4, C3H6, C4H8, C5H10, C6H12. H = 2 × C → CnH2n.
  • Alkynes ആൽക്കൈനുകൾ: at least one C≡C. Ethyne C2H2, CH≡CH (Fig 8.6). Table 8.4: C2H2, C3H4, C4H6, C5H8, C6H10. H = 2 × C − 2 → CnH2n−2.
  • Classify by n: alkanes C5H12, C9H20, C3H8; alkenes C5H10, C2H4, C6H12, C4H8; alkynes C3H4, C7H12, C4H6.
