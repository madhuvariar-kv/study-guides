📖 What you need (pages 3–9 of this guide)
  • Hydrocarbons ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണുകൾ: only C and H; carbon always makes 4 bonds (page 3).
  • Alkanes ആൽക്കെയ്നുകൾ CnH2n+2 · alkenes ആൽക്കീനുകൾ CnH2n · alkynes ആൽക്കൈനുകൾ CnH2n−2 (page 4, page 5).
  • Homologous series ഹോമലോഗസ് ശ്രേണി: neighbours differ by –CH2– (page 6). IUPAC names: root + ane / ene / yne (page 7).
  • Ring compounds വലയസംയുക്തങ്ങൾ: cyclo + name; benzene and naphthalene (page 8). Fuels and global warming ആഗോളതാപനം (page 9).

Question 1 · Read a structural formula

1. ഒരു ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണിന്റെ ഘടനാവാക്യം നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു. The structural formula ഘടനാവാക്യം of a hydrocarbon is given.

  1. കണ്ടൻസ്ഡ് ഫോർമുല എഴുതുക. Write its condensed formula കണ്ടൻസ്ഡ് ഫോർമുല.
  2. തന്മാത്രാസൂത്രം എഴുതുക. Write its molecular formula തന്മാത്രാസൂത്രം.
  3. ഈ ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബൺ ഉൾപ്പെട്ട ഹോമലോഗസ് ശ്രേണിയിലെ ആദ്യ സംയുക്തത്തിന്റെ ഘടന വരയ്ക്കുക. Draw the structure of the first member of the homologous series this hydrocarbon belongs to.
  4. ഈ സംയുക്തത്തിന്റെ IUPAC നാമം എഴുതുക. Write the IUPAC name of this compound.
a) Condensed formula: CH3–CH2–CH=CH2 (the same molecule can be written CH2=CH–CH2–CH3).
b) Count: 4 C; H = 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 = 8 → C4H8. Check: 8 = 2 × 4, so it fits CnH2n.
c) It has one C=C, so it is an alkene. The first alkene has 2 carbons (there is no 1-carbon alkene, because a double bond needs two carbons): ethene ഈഥീൻ, C2H4, H2C=CH2 – drawn below.
d) 4 carbons → root but; double bond → suffix ene: butene go further but-1-ene (the double bond starts at carbon 1).

Question 6 · Three carbons, CnH2n+2

Question 7 · A ring with C6H12

7. ഒരു വലയ സംയുക്തത്തെ കുറിച്ചുള്ള സൂചന ചുവടെ നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു. A clue about a ring compound: • it has 6 carbon atoms and 12 hydrogen atoms.

  1. ഈ സംയുക്തത്തിന്റെ ഘടന വരയ്ക്കുക. Draw its structure.
  2. ഇതേ തന്മാത്രാസൂത്രമുള്ള ഓപ്പൺ ചെയിൻ ഹൈഡ്രോകാർബണിന്റെ ഘടനാവാക്യം എഴുതുക. Write the structural formula of an open-chain ഓപ്പൺ ചെയിൻ hydrocarbon with the same molecular formula.
  3. ഇത്രയും എണ്ണം കാർബൺ ആറ്റങ്ങൾ അടങ്ങിയ ആൽക്കെയ്നിന്റെ തന്മാത്രാസൂത്രം എഴുതുക. Write the molecular formula of the alkane with the same number of carbons.
a) Six carbons in a ring, all single bonds, two H on each carbon: cyclohexane സൈക്ലോഹെക്സെയ്ൻ, C6H12 (left).
b) An open chain of 6 carbons with 12 H has H = 2n, so it needs one double bond: hexene, CH2=CH–CH2–CH2–CH2–CH3 (right). Closing the ring uses up the same “two hydrogens” that a double bond does.
c) Hexane: H = 2 × 6 + 2 = 14 → C6H14.

Question 14 · Global warming

14. a) ചുവടെ നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നവയിൽ ആഗോളതാപനത്തിന് കാരണമാകാത്ത വാതകം ഏത്? (മീഥെയ്ൻ, കാർബൺ ഡൈഓക്സൈഡ്, നൈട്രജൻ, നൈട്രസ് ഓക്സൈഡ്) Which of these gases does not cause global warming ആഗോളതാപനം? (methane, carbon dioxide, nitrogen, nitrous oxide)
b) ആഗോളതാപനം തടയുന്നതിനുള്ള ഏതെങ്കിലും രണ്ട് മാർഗങ്ങൾ എഴുതുക. Write any two ways to prevent global warming.

a) Nitrogen (N2). Methane (CH4), carbon dioxide (CO2) and nitrous oxide (N2O) are greenhouse gases ഹരിതഗൃഹവാതകങ്ങൾ: they trap the infrared rays ഇൻഫ്രാറെഡ് രശ്മികൾ given out by the warm Earth. Nitrogen, 78 % of the air, does not.
b) Any two (with a reason earns full marks):
  • Burn less fossil fuel ഫോസിൽ ഇന്ധനം (coal, petrol, diesel) – less CO2.
  • Use renewable energy: solar panels, wind power.
  • Plant trees and protect forests and wetlands – plants take in CO2.
  • Use public transport, cycles, CNG or electric vehicles; save electricity.
  • Use CFC-free fridges and air conditioners.
⚠️ Four traps in these questions
  • Count the bonds on every carbon: always 4. A C=C counts as two, C≡C as three (Q1, Q8).
  • The suffix tells the bond: -ane single, -ene double, -yne triple. Propane C3H8 ≠ propene C3H6 (Q6, Q11).
  • A ring uses up two hydrogens, just like a double bond: cyclohexane and hexene are both C6H12 (Q7–Q11). Do not forget the cyclo prefix.
  • Nitrogen is not a greenhouse gas; nitrous oxide (N2O) is (Q14).

Extended activity 1 · Ball-and-stick models of butane, butene and butyne

1. 4 കാർബൺ ആറ്റങ്ങളുള്ള ആൽക്കെയ്ൻ, ആൽക്കീൻ, ആൽക്കൈൻ സംയുക്തങ്ങളുടെ ബോൾ ആന്റ് സ്റ്റിക്ക് മാതൃക നിർമ്മിച്ച് പ്രദർശിപ്പിക്കുക. Make and display ball-and-stick models of the alkane, alkene and alkyne with 4 carbon atoms.

🧱 Model builder – pick a molecule and turn it

The real angles are used: about 109.5° around a carbon with four single bonds, 120° around a C=C carbon, and a straight line along C≡C. In butyne, four atoms H–C≡C–C lie on one straight line.

You need: 12 dark balls (carbon) and 24 small white balls (hydrogen) – clay or play-dough, thermocol balls, or beads; toothpicks or broom sticks cut short; a little glue. Make the carbon balls bigger than the hydrogen balls.
  1. Butane C4H10: join 4 carbon balls in a zigzag with single sticks. Stick 3 H on each end carbon and 2 H on each middle carbon (10 H). Space the four sticks of each carbon evenly in 3-D, like a tripod with one leg straight up.
  2. Butene C4H8: join carbon 1 and carbon 2 with two sticks bent slightly (or a pair of flexible pipe-cleaners) – the double bond. Then add 2 H on C1, 1 H on C2, 2 H on C3 and 3 H on C4 (8 H). The C=C part is flat.
  3. Butyne C4H6: join carbon 1 and carbon 2 with three flexible sticks – the triple bond. Add 1 H on C1, no H on C2, 2 H on C3 and 3 H on C4 (6 H). Keep H–C≡C–C in a straight line.
  4. Check every carbon: count the sticks – it must be 4 (a double bond counts 2, a triple bond 3). Label each model with its name, molecular formula and family.

Extended activity 3 · Essay: the importance of organic chemistry

3. 'കാർബണിക രസതന്ത്രത്തിന്റെ പ്രാധാന്യം' എന്ന വിഷയത്തെ അടിസ്ഥാനമാക്കി ഒരു പ്രബന്ധം തയ്യാറാക്കി അവതരിപ്പിക്കുക. Prepare and present an essay on “The importance of organic chemistry” കാർബണിക രസതന്ത്രം.

The importance of organic chemistry – outline
Introduction. Organic chemistry is the chemistry of carbon compounds (except CO, CO2, carbonates and bicarbonates). Berzelius thought they could be made only by living things (vital force ജീവശക്തി), but in 1828 Wöhler made urea in the laboratory. Today we know millions of carbon compounds – many times more than the compounds of all other elements together – because of catenation കാറ്റിനേഷൻ and carbon's valency of four.
1. Life itself. Carbohydrates (rice, wheat, sugar from sugarcane), proteins, fats, vitamins and DNA are all organic compounds.
2. Food and agriculture. Food preservatives, flavours, pesticides and some fertilisers.
3. Medicines. Painkillers, antibiotics and vaccines are made and tested by organic chemists.
4. Fuels. LPG (butane), petrol, diesel, kerosene, CNG (methane) – all hydrocarbons from petroleum, coal and natural gas.
5. Materials. Plastics (polythene from ethene), synthetic fibres (nylon, polyester), rubber, paints, dyes, soaps and detergents, cosmetics (vaseline).
6. Responsibility. Burning fossil fuels causes global warming; plastics pollute. Chemists are making biodegradable plastics and cleaner fuels.
Conclusion. From the food we eat to the clothes we wear and the medicines that save us, organic chemistry is everywhere; using it wisely is our duty.

Extended activity 4 · Are these two homologues?

4. ചില ഓർഗാനിക് സംയുക്തങ്ങളുടെ ഘടനാവാക്യങ്ങൾ ചുവടെ നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു. The structural formulae of some organic compounds are given: i) CH3–CH2–OH   ii) CH3–CH2–CH2–OH

  1. ഈ സംയുക്തങ്ങളുടെ തന്മാത്രാസൂത്രങ്ങൾ എഴുതുക. Write their molecular formulae.
  2. ഇവ ഹോമലോഗ് ആണോ? നിങ്ങളുടെ ഉത്തരം സാധൂകരിക്കുക. Are they homologues? Justify your answer.
🔎 Both molecules fully drawn (oxygen in red: 2 bonds)
a) i) C 2, H 3 + 2 + 1 = 6, O 1 → C2H6O (also written C2H5OH). ii) C 3, H 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 8, O 1 → C3H8O (C3H7OH).
b) Yes, they are homologues. C3H8O − C2H6O = CH2: they differ by one –CH2– group, and both have the same –OH group, so they have similar chemical properties and fit one general formula. go further They are alcohols, CnH2n+1OH: ethanol and propanol.

🔑 The ideas that answered everything on this page

Carbon always has 4 bonds. Alkanes CnH2n+2 (-ane), alkenes CnH2n (-ene), alkynes CnH2n−2 (-yne); word roots meth, eth, prop, but, pent, hex. Homologues differ by –CH2–. A ring takes two H away, like a double bond: cycloalkane CnH2n. Benzene C6H6, naphthalene C10H8. Petroleum is separated by fractional distillation; LPG is mainly butane. CO2, CH4, N2O and CFCs cause global warming; N2 does not.
