All ten end-of-chapter questions with model answers. Equations are balanced step by step with a live atom tally, and the oxidation numbers are written above the symbols just as in the textbook, with arrows showing which atom is oxidised and which is reduced. Try every question yourself first: there are true/false buttons, a balancer, fill-in tables and a concept map you can fill by tapping or dragging. 📖 What you need (pages 3–8 of this guide)
  • Law of conservation of mass മാസ് സംരക്ഷണനിയമം: total mass of reactants = total mass of products (page 3).
  • Balancing രാസസമവാക്യ സമീകരണം: change only the coefficients (numbers in front), never the subscripts; count each kind of atom on both sides (page 4).
  • Oxidation ഓക്സീകരണം = loss of electrons = increase in oxidation number. Reduction നിരോക്സീകരണം = gain of electrons = decrease in oxidation number (pages 5, 8).
  • The oxidising agent ഓക്സീകാരി is itself reduced; the reducing agent നിരോക്സീകാരി is itself oxidised (page 6).
  • Oxidation number ഓക്സിഡേഷൻ നമ്പർ: elemental state മൂലകാവസ്ഥ = 0; the sum in a compound = 0; H +1, Na +1, K +1, Ca +2, Al +3, F/Cl/Br/I −1, O −2 (Table 4.7, page 7).

Oxidation numbers appear in small red numbers above the symbols. In the animations a green arrow above means the number went up (oxidation) and a red arrow below means it went down (reduction). A changed coefficient is shown in red. Parts marked go further are beyond the textbook.

Question 1 · Ammonia from nitrogen and hydrogen

1. നൈട്രജനും ഹൈഡ്രജനും സംയോജിച്ച് അമോണിയ ഉണ്ടാകുന്ന രാസപ്രവർത്തനത്തിന്റെ സമീകരിക്കാത്ത സമവാക്യം നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു. The unbalanced equation for the reaction in which nitrogen and hydrogen combine to form ammonia is given: N₂ + H₂ → NH₃

  1. രാസസമവാക്യം സമീകരിക്കുക. Balance the chemical equation.
  2. അഭികാരകങ്ങളിലെയും ഉൽപന്നങ്ങളിലെയും ഒരേയിനത്തിൽപ്പെട്ട ആറ്റങ്ങളുടെ എണ്ണം കണക്കാക്കുക. Count the atoms of each kind in the reactants അഭികാരകങ്ങൾ and in the products ഉൽപന്നങ്ങൾ.
  3. 28 ഗ്രാം നൈട്രജൻ 6 ഗ്രാം ഹൈഡ്രജനുമായി പൂർണമായി സംയോജിക്കുന്നെങ്കിൽ ഉണ്ടാകുന്ന അമോണിയയുടെ മാസ് എത്രയായിരിക്കും? (സൂചന: അറ്റോമിക മാസ് H = 1u, N = 14u) If 28 g of nitrogen combines completely with 6 g of hydrogen, what will be the mass of ammonia formed? (Hint: atomic mass H = 1 u, N = 14 u.)

Balance N first (put a number in front of NH₃), then H. For (c) think of Lavoisier: mass is neither created nor destroyed.

🎬 Step by step – balance, count, then weigh
(a) N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃
(b)
AtomReactantsProducts
N2 (in N₂)2 (in 2NH₃)
H6 (in 3H₂)6 (in 2NH₃)
N: 2 = 2; H: 6 = 6. The equation is balanced.
(c) By the law of conservation of mass, the total mass of the products equals the total mass of the reactants: 28 g + 6 g = 34 g of ammonia. Check with atomic masses: N₂ = 2 × 14 = 28 u; 3H₂ = 3 × 2 = 6 u; 2NH₃ = 2 × (14 + 3) = 2 × 17 = 34 u. So 28 g of N₂ needs exactly 6 g of H₂, and gives 34 g of NH₃.

Question 2 · Carbon and nitric acid

2. C + 4HNO₃ → 2H₂O + CO₂ + 4NO₂

  1. ഈ രാസസമവാക്യത്തിൽ കാർബണിന്റെ ഓക്സിഡേഷൻ നമ്പറുകൾ കണ്ടെത്തി രേഖപ്പെടുത്തുക. Find and write the oxidation numbers of carbon in this equation.
  2. ഈ രാസപ്രവർത്തനത്തിൽ കാർബണിന്റെ ഓക്സിഡേഷൻ നമ്പറിന് എന്താണ് സംഭവിക്കുന്നത്? What happens to the oxidation number of carbon in this reaction?
  3. കാർബണിന് ഓക്സീകരണമാണോ നിരോക്സീകരണമാണോ സംഭവിക്കുന്നത്? Does carbon undergo oxidation or reduction?
  4. ഈ രാസപ്രവർത്തനത്തിലെ ഓക്സീകാരിയേത്? നിരോക്സീകാരിയേത്? Which is the oxidising agent in this reaction? Which is the reducing agent?
🎬 Step by step – oxidation numbers above every atom
(a) Before the reaction carbon is in the elemental state: {0|C}, oxidation number 0. After: in CO₂, x + 2(−2) = 0, so x = +4: {+4|C}{−2|O}₂.
(b) The oxidation number of carbon increases from 0 to +4 (by 4).
(c) An increase in oxidation number is oxidation. Carbon is oxidised.
(d) Nitrogen: in HNO₃, (+1) + x + 3(−2) = 0, x = +5; in NO₂, x + 2(−2) = 0, x = +4. Nitrogen goes +5 → +4, a decrease, so it is reduced. H (+1) and O (−2) do not change.
Oxidising agent: HNO₃ (nitric acid; it is reduced). Reducing agent: C (carbon; it is oxidised).
{0|C} + 4{+1|H}{+5|N}{−2|O}₃ → 2{+1|H}₂{−2|O} + {+4|C}{−2|O}₂ + 4{+4|N}{−2|O}₂

Question 4 · True or false?

4. ചില പ്രസ്താവനകൾ നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു. ഇവ ശരിയോ തെറ്റോ എന്നെഴുതുക. Some statements are given. Write whether each is true or false.

👆 Tap True or False for each statement
a) ഓക്സിഡേഷൻ നമ്പർ കൂടുന്ന പ്രവർത്തനമാണ് ഓക്സീകരണം. Oxidation is the process in which the oxidation number increases.
b) ഓക്സിഡേഷൻ നമ്പർ കുറയുന്ന പ്രവർത്തനമാണ് ഓക്സീകരണം. Oxidation is the process in which the oxidation number decreases.
c) ഒരു രാസപ്രവർത്തനത്തിൽ ഓക്സീകാരി നിരോക്സീകരിക്കപ്പെടുന്നു. In a chemical reaction the oxidising agent is reduced.
d) ഒരു രാസപ്രവർത്തനത്തിൽ ഓക്സീകാരി ഓക്സീകരിക്കപ്പെടുന്നു. In a chemical reaction the oxidising agent is oxidised.

a) True. b) False: a decrease in oxidation number is reduction. c) True. d) False: the oxidising agent is reduced (the reducing agent is the one that is oxidised).
Memory aid: “Loss = Oxidation, Gain = Reduction” of electrons; the oxidation number goes up in oxidation and down in reduction; the oxidising agent takes electrons (so it is reduced), and the reducing agent gives electrons (so it is oxidised).

Question 5 · Balance four equations

5. ചുവടെ നൽകിയിട്ടുള്ള രാസസമവാക്യങ്ങൾ സമീകരിക്കുക. Balance the chemical equations below.

a) SO₂ + O₂ → SO₃    b) H₂O₂ → H₂O + O₂
c) CH₄ + O₂ → H₂O + CO₂    d) Fe + HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂

⚖️ Equation balancer – change only the coefficients

Press + or − under each formula. Each tally box turns green when that atom has the same count on both sides. The subscripts are locked: they belong to the formula of the substance.

a) 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃   (S 2 = 2; O 4 + 2 = 6 = 2 × 3)
b) 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂   (H 4 = 4; O 4 = 2 + 2)
c) CH₄ + 2O₂ → 2H₂O + CO₂   (C 1 = 1; H 4 = 4; O 4 = 2 + 2)
d) Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂   (Fe 1 = 1; H 2 = 2; Cl 2 = 2)
Method: balance the atom that appears in the fewest formulae first; leave O₂, H₂ and single elements for last; then recount every atom.

Question 6 · Redox or not? (I)

6. രണ്ടു രാസസമവാക്യങ്ങൾ നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു. ആറ്റങ്ങളുടെ ഓക്സിഡേഷൻ നമ്പർ കണ്ടെത്തി ഇവ റിഡോക്സ് പ്രവർത്തനമാണോയെന്ന് പരിശോധിക്കുക. Two chemical equations are given. Find the oxidation numbers of the atoms and check whether these are redox reactions റിഡോക്സ് പ്രവർത്തനങ്ങൾ.

a) CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂    b) Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂

🔍 Redox detective
a) {+2|Ca}{+4|C}{−2|O}₃ + 2{+1|H}{−1|Cl} → {+2|Ca}{−1|Cl}₂ + {+1|H}₂{−2|O} + {+4|C}{−2|O}₂
C in CaCO₃: (+2) + x + 3(−2) = 0 → x = +4. Ca +2 → +2, C +4 → +4, O −2 → −2, H +1 → +1, Cl −1 → −1. No oxidation number changes, so this is not a redox reaction.
b) {0|Zn} + 2{+1|H}{−1|Cl} → {+2|Zn}{−1|Cl}₂ + {0|H}₂
Zn 0 → +2 (increase: oxidised); H +1 → 0 (decrease: reduced); Cl −1 → −1. Oxidation and reduction take place together, so this is a redox reaction. Oxidising agent HCl, reducing agent Zn.

Question 7 · Carbon monoxide burns

7. ഒരു ഇന്ധനമായ കാർബൺ മോണോക്സൈഡ് (CO), ഓക്സിജനിൽ കത്തി കാർബൺ ഡൈഓക്സൈഡ് ഉണ്ടാകുന്നു. Carbon monoxide (CO), a fuel, burns in oxygen to form carbon dioxide.

  1. ഈ രാസപ്രവർത്തനത്തിന്റെ സമീകരിച്ച സമവാക്യം എഴുതുക. Write the balanced equation for this reaction.
  2. ഈ രാസപ്രവർത്തനം റിഡോക്സ് പ്രവർത്തനമാണോ? എന്തുകൊണ്ട്? Is this a redox reaction? Why?
  3. ഈ പ്രവർത്തനത്തിലെ ഓക്സീകാരി ഏത്? നിരോക്സീകാരി ഏത്? Which is the oxidising agent? Which is the reducing agent?
🎬 Step by step – balance first, then the oxidation numbers
(a) 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ (C 2 = 2; O 2 + 2 = 4 = 2 × 2).
(b) Yes, it is a redox reaction. 2{+2|C}{−2|O} + {0|O}₂ → 2{+4|C}{−2|O}₂. C in CO: x + (−2) = 0, x = +2; in CO₂: +4. Carbon's oxidation number increases (+2 → +4): oxidation. Oxygen of O₂ goes 0 → −2: reduction. Oxidation and reduction happen at the same time, so it is redox.
(c) Oxidising agent: O₂ (it is reduced). Reducing agent: CO (its carbon is oxidised).

Question 9 · Redox or not? (II)

9. ചുവടെ നൽകിയിട്ടുള്ള രാസസമവാക്യങ്ങൾ വിലയിരുത്തി റിഡോക്സ് പ്രവർത്തനമാണോയെന്ന് കണ്ടെത്തുക. ഉത്തരം സാധൂകരിക്കുക. Evaluate the chemical equations below and find out whether they are redox reactions. Justify your answer.

a) NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H₂O    b) H₂S + Cl₂ → 2HCl + S

🔍 Redox detective
a) {+1|Na}{−2|O}{+1|H} + {+1|H}{−1|Cl} → {+1|Na}{−1|Cl} + {+1|H}₂{−2|O}
Na +1, O −2, H +1 and Cl −1 are the same on both sides. No oxidation number changes: not a redox reaction. (It is a neutralisation: an acid and a base give a salt and water.)
b) {+1|H}₂{−2|S} + {0|Cl}₂ → 2{+1|H}{−1|Cl} + {0|S}
S −2 → 0 (increase: oxidation); Cl 0 → −1 (decrease: reduction); H stays +1. Both happen together: a redox reaction. Oxidising agent Cl₂; reducing agent H₂S.

⚠️ Four traps in these questions
  • Never change a subscript to balance (H₂O → H₂O₂ makes a different substance).
  • Elements in the free state (Fe, S, C, Zn, O₂, H₂, Cl₂) always have oxidation number 0.
  • Multiply by the number of atoms: H₂SO₄ has 2 × (+1) and 4 × (−2).
  • Name the agent as the whole substance on the left: the oxidising agent in Q2 is HNO₃, in Q8 HCl.

Equation: valencies Fe 2, S 2 → Fe₂S₂ → FeS. Fe + S → FeS (balanced: Fe 1 = 1, S 1 = 1). The ratio 7 : 4 is the mass ratio 56 : 32 of one Fe atom (56 u) to one S atom (32 u), so all the iron and all the sulphur react.
Redox? {0|Fe} + {0|S} → {+2|Fe}{−2|S}. Fe 0 → +2 (oxidised), S 0 → −2 (reduced). Yes, it is a redox reaction; S is the oxidising agent and Fe the reducing agent (the same as the concept map in Q10).

CaC₂ + 2H₂O → C₂H₂ + Ca(OH)₂ (gives the gas).
Combustion: C₂H₂ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O, balanced: 2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O (C 4 = 4; H 4 = 4; O 10 = 8 + 2).
Redox? 2{−1|C}₂{+1|H}₂ + 5{0|O}₂ → 4{+4|C}{−2|O}₂ + 2{+1|H}₂{−2|O}. C in C₂H₂: 2x + 2(+1) = 0 → x = −1; in CO₂ +4. Carbon −1 → +4 (oxidised), oxygen 0 → −2 (reduced), H stays +1. Yes, the combustion is a redox reaction; O₂ is the oxidising agent, C₂H₂ the reducing agent.
Go further: in CaC₂ + 2H₂O → C₂H₂ + Ca(OH)₂ no oxidation number changes (C stays −1, Ca +2, H +1, O −2), so that first step is not redox.

Extended activity 4 · A field trip on redox in industry

4. റിഡോക്സ് പ്രവർത്തനങ്ങളുടെ വ്യാവസായിക പ്രാധാന്യം നേരിട്ട് മനസ്സിലാക്കുക എന്ന ലക്ഷ്യത്തോടെ ഒരു പഠനയാത്ര സംഘടിപ്പിക്കുക. Organise a field trip with the aim of understanding the industrial importance of redox reactions at first hand.

🏭 Where redox works for industry
  • Extraction of metals: metal ores are oxides or sulphides; the metal ions are reduced to the metal (iron from its oxide with carbon).
  • Electroplating and galvanising: metal ions gain electrons (reduction) and coat an object; zinc on GI sheets protects iron from rusting.
  • Batteries and cells രാസവൈദ്യുത സെല്ലുകൾ: one substance is oxidised and another reduced, and the electrons flow through the wire.
  • Bleaching and water treatment: chlorine and potassium permanganate act as oxidising agents.
  • Rust prevention: paint, oil and zinc coatings keep oxygen and moisture away from iron.

🔑 The ideas that answered everything on this page

Balance with coefficients only. Write oxidation numbers above every atom: elements 0, sum in a compound 0. Up = oxidation, down = reduction; both together = redox. The substance containing the atom that goes down is the oxidising agent; the one containing the atom that goes up is the reducing agent. No change anywhere = not redox.
