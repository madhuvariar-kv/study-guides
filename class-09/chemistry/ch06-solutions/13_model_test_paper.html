18.
Read the passage and answer the questions.
Shameer runs a juice shop near Kozhikode beach. For one glass of kulukki sarbath he puts into a tall shaker: crushed ice, a spoonful of soaked basil seeds (kasakasa), a pinch of salt, the juice of half a lime with the lime pieces dropped in, a slit green chilli and some sugar syrup. He tops it up with cold soda water, covers the shaker with a glass and shakes it hard. He makes the sugar syrup every morning by dissolving 600 g of sugar in 400 g of hot water. On his shelf there are also bottles of a lemon-flavoured soft drink. Its label reads: Ingredients: carbonated water, sugar, lemon flavour, stabiliser (glyceryl ester of rosin), preservative (sodium benzoate), colour (tartrazine). Best before 6 months from packing.
(a) Name two substances in the sarbath that dissolve in water and two that do not.
(b) Find the mass percentage of sugar in Shameer’s syrup.
(c) For a less sweet drink he mixes 100 g of the syrup with 500 g of water. What is the mass percentage of sugar now?
(d) Why are a stabiliser and a preservative added to the bottled drink?
(e) The sarbath must be drunk soon after it is made, but the bottled drink lasts for months. Which is the better choice for health? Give one reason.
