1. The chapter in one picture

🗺️ Sound – concept map

Read it clockwise from the top left: a source vibrates; the vibrations travel through a medium (never through vacuum) at a speed that depends on the medium; the compressions and rarefactions make our eardrum vibrate and the brain perceives sound; regular vibrations give music, irregular ones noise; too much unwanted sound is noise pollution.

2. The key boxes of the textbook

1Sound (p. 157)ശബ്ദം · ഊർജരൂപം · ശ്രവണബോധം

Sound is a form of energy that produces the sensation of hearing. – page 5

2Vibration (p. 159)കമ്പനം · ശബ്ദസ്രോതസ്സുകൾ

Sound is produced by the vibration of objects. Objects that produce sound are sources of sound. – page 5

3Main and associated parts (p. 160)പ്രധാന ഭാഗം · അനുബന്ധ ഭാഗങ്ങൾ

Every source of sound has a main part that vibrates. The sound we hear is the combined effect of the vibrations of several parts and of the surrounding air. In musical instruments, the main part and the associated parts together make the sound sweeter and louder. – page 5

4Not through vacuum (p. 162)ശബ്ദപ്രേഷണം · ശൂന്യത

Sound reaches our ears by travelling through air. Sound cannot travel through vacuum. – page 6

5A medium is needed (p. 163)മാധ്യമം · ഖരം · ദ്രാവകം · വാതകം

Sound can travel through gases, liquids and solids. Sound needs a medium to travel. – page 6

6Speed of sound (p. 164)ശബ്ദവേഗം · താപനില
solids > liquids > gases

The speed of sound is different in different media, and it increases when the temperature of the medium increases. – page 7

7How we hear (p. 165)മർദവ്യതിയാനങ്ങൾ · കർണ്ണപുടം · ശ്രവണേന്ദ്രിയം

A vibrating object makes rapid pressure variations in the air; they travel through the medium and make the eardrum vibrate; the vibrations reach the inner ear and signals go to the brain. We need a source, a medium and an organ of hearing. – page 8

8Music and noise (p. 167)സംഗീതം · ഒച്ച

Music: sound from regular vibrations, pleasant to hear. Noise: sound from irregular vibrations, unpleasant. – page 9

9Noise pollution (p. 168)ശബ്ദമലിനീകരണം

Unnecessary sound that is unbearable and uncomfortable and that harms humans and other living things is noise pollution. – page 9

How sound travelscompressions (C, high pressure) and rarefactions (R, low pressure); the particles only vibrate to and fro – they do not travel with the sound

Hearing lossfound with an audiometer; a hearing aid helps; World Hearing Day is 3 March; treat people with hearing loss with kindness and include them

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Saying sound can travel through vacuum or in space. It cannot – that is why astronauts use radio.
  • Thinking the air particles travel from the source to the ear. They only vibrate to and fro about their places; the disturbance (the compressions and rarefactions) travels.
  • Saying sound is fastest in gases. It is slowest in gases and fastest in solids (air 347 m/s, aluminium 6423 m/s).
  • Saying the speed of sound decreases with temperature. It increases.
  • Forgetting that light reaches us almost instantly. In thunder and firecracker sums the whole delay is the time taken by the sound.
  • Naming only the main vibrating part and forgetting the associated parts (or the other way round) in Table 8.1 questions.
  • Writing “noise” for every loud sound and “music” for every soft sound. The difference is regular vs irregular vibration – and loud music can become noise.
  • Putting objects into the ear to clean it. Pins, sticks and deep cotton buds can tear the eardrum; wounds there heal slowly and get infected.
