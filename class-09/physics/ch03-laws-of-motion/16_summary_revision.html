1. The chapter in one picture

🗺️ Laws of motion – concept map

Read it clockwise from the top left: forces add up to a resultant; only an unbalanced external force changes motion (first law); how much it changes the momentum is the second law; a big force for a short time is impulse; and forces always come in action–reaction pairs (third law).

2. The key boxes of the textbook

1Resultant force (p. 54)ഒരു വസ്തുവിൽ ഒന്നിലധികം ബലങ്ങൾ ഒരേ സമയത്ത് പ്രയോഗിക്കുമ്പോൾ ഈ ബലങ്ങൾ വസ്തുവിൽ ഉളവാക്കുന്ന ആകെ ബലമാണ് സഫലബലം അഥവാ പരിണതബലം.

When several forces act on an object at the same time, the total force they produce is the resultant force. Same direction: add; opposite directions: subtract (take one direction as +). – page 5

2Balanced forces (p. 55)സന്തുലിതബലങ്ങൾ

Forces whose resultant is zero. They cannot move a body at rest, nor change the speed or direction of a moving body. – page 5

3Unbalanced forces (p. 55)അസന്തുലിതബലങ്ങൾ

Forces whose resultant is not zero. They can move a body at rest and change the speed or direction of a moving body. – page 5

4Unbalanced external force (p. 56)അസന്തുലിത ബാഹ്യബലം

An unbalanced external force can change an object’s state of rest or motion, its direction or speed, or create a tendency to do so; the change is in the direction of the unbalanced force. – page 5

5Internal forces (p. 57)ആന്തരികബലം

All internal forces are balanced forces – that is why pushing an autorickshaw from inside cannot move it. – page 5

6Galileo’s conclusion (p. 58)ഗലീലിയോയുടെ നിരീക്ഷണങ്ങൾ

To stop a moving object, an unbalanced external force must act opposite to its motion. No unbalanced external force is needed to keep an object moving uniformly in a straight line. – page 6

7Changing the state (p. 58)

An unbalanced external force is needed to change an object’s state of motion or rest. – page 6

8Newton’s first law (p. 59)ന്യൂട്ടന്റെ ഒന്നാം ചലനനിയമം

Every object continues in its state of rest or of uniform motion in a straight line unless acted on by an unbalanced external force. Force is what changes, or tends to change, that state. – page 6

9Inertia (p. 59)ജഡത്വം · നിശ്ചലജഡത്വം · ചലനജഡത്വം

The tendency of an object to continue in its state of rest or motion: inertia of rest and inertia of motion. Inertia increases with mass. – page 7

10Momentum (p. 63)മൊമെന്റം
p = mv  (kg m/s)

The product of the mass and the velocity of a moving object. A vector, in the direction of the velocity. – page 8

11Newton’s second law (p. 64)ന്യൂട്ടന്റെ രണ്ടാം ചലനനിയമം
F = m(v − u)/t = ma

The rate of change of momentum of an object is directly proportional to the unbalanced external force on it, and the change is in the direction of the force. With the newton as unit, k = 1. – page 9

12Impulsive force and impulse (p. 67)ആവേഗബലം · ബലത്തിന്റെ ആവേഗം
I = F × t  (N s)

A large force acting for a very short time is an impulsive force; force × time is its impulse. – page 11

13Impulse–momentum principle (p. 67)ആവേഗമൊമെന്റതത്വം
Ft = mv − mu

The impulse of a force equals the change in momentum it produces. Same Δp, longer t → smaller F. – page 11

14Newton’s third law (p. 68)ന്യൂട്ടന്റെ മൂന്നാം ചലനനിയമം

For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. – page 12

15Action and reaction (p. 69)ബലവും പ്രതിബലവും
F12 = −F21

They act at the same time, on different objects, and only in pairs: if there is an action, there is a reaction. – page 12

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Adding forces in opposite directions without signs. 100 N east and 150 N west is not 250 N: 100 + (−150) = −50 N, i.e. 50 N towards the west.
  • Forgetting to convert km/h to m/s (× 5/18) or g to kg (÷ 1000) before using a formula.
  • Taking the change in momentum of a rebounding ball as 0. A 200 g ball hitting a stick at 20 m/s and coming back at 20 m/s: 0.2 × (−20) − 0.2 × 20 = −8 kg m/s.
  • “Action and reaction cancel.” They act on different bodies, so they never cancel.
  • “A moving object needs a force to keep moving.” A force is needed only to change the motion (Galileo, first law); friction is what stops things.
  • Mixing up mass, inertia and weight; thinking inertia depends on speed. Inertia depends only on mass.
  • Dropping the negative sign, or not explaining it. −5000 N means the force acts opposite to the direction of motion (a braking force).
  • Mixing up units: momentum kg m/s, force N = kg m/s2, impulse N s.
  • Saying a body in uniform circular motion has balanced forces. Its direction keeps changing, so an unbalanced force acts (a satellite: gravity).
