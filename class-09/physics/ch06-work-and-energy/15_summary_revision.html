1. The chapter in one picture

🗺️ Work and energy – concept map

Read it clockwise from the top left: work is force × displacement; doing work against gravity stores potential energy; energy is the capacity to do work; mechanical energy is potential + kinetic; energy only changes form (conservation); power tells how fast the work is done; and almost all our energy comes from the Sun – through food, fuels, rain and solar cells.

2. The key boxes of the textbook

1Work (p. 108)പ്രവൃത്തി · ബലം · സ്ഥാനാന്തരം

When a force acts on an object and the object is displaced in the direction of the force, the force is said to have done work on it. No displacement → no work (holding a sack still, pushing a rock that does not move). – page 5

2Amount of work (p. 109)ജൂൾ (J) · ന്യൂട്ടൺ മീറ്റർ
W = F × s

Work = force × displacement in the direction of the force. Unit: N × m = newton metre = joule (J), after James Prescott Joule. – page 5

3One joule (p. 110)ജൂൾ (J)
1 J = 1 N × 1 m

1 J is the work done when a force of 1 N moves an object through 1 m in the direction of the force. – page 5

4Work against gravity (p. 111)ഗുരുത്വാകർഷണത്തിനെതിരായ പ്രവൃത്തി
W = mgh

Lifting a mass m through a height h needs a force mg upwards, so W = mg × h. It depends only on the height, not on the path (straight up or along a ramp). – page 6

5Positive and negative work (p. 112)പോസിറ്റീവ് പ്രവൃത്തി / നെഗറ്റീവ് പ്രവൃത്തി

Work is positive when the displacement is in the direction of the force (your push on a trolley, gravity on a falling coconut) and negative when it is opposite (friction, gravity on a stone thrown up). A force at right angles to the motion does no work. – page 6

6Energy (p. 113)ഊർജം · കലോറി / കിലോകലോറി

Energy is the capacity to do work. SI unit: joule. Food energy in calories: 1 cal = 4.2 J, 1 kcal = 4200 J (1000 cal = 1 kcal). – page 7

7Mechanical energy (p. 114)യാന്ത്രികോർജം

The energy an object has because of its motion, position or configuration. Two kinds: potential energy and kinetic energy. – page 7

8Potential energy (p. 114)സ്ഥിതികോർജം
Ep = mgh

Energy due to position (coconut on the tree, water in a tank or dam) or configuration (compressed spring, drawn bow, bent pole of a pole vaulter). h is measured from the chosen zero level (the ground). – page 7

9Kinetic energy (p. 116)ഗതികോർജം
Ek = ½mv2

Energy due to motion. Mass doubled → KE doubled; velocity doubled → 4 times; tripled → 9 times; halved → ¼. – page 8

10Conservation of energy (p. 121)ഊർജസംരക്ഷണ നിയമം · ഊർജപരിവർത്തനം

Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can be changed from one form into another without any loss or gain. Work and energy are both scalars with the same unit; energy is used up when work is done. – page 9

11Power (p. 124)പവർ · വാട്ട് (W) · കുതിരശക്തി (HP)
P = W ÷ t

Power is the work done per unit time – the rate of doing work. Unit J/s = watt (W), after James Watt. 1 kW = 103 W, 1 MW = 106 W, 1 HP = 746 W. – page 10

⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Saying work is done when holding a load still or pushing a wall. No displacement → no work, however tired you feel.
  • Using the slanted length of a ramp instead of the vertical height in mgh. Work against gravity depends only on h (the barrel: 1000 J either way).
  • Forgetting to square v in ½mv², or squaring the ½ or the m. Only v is squared: ½ × 6 × 12² = 3 × 144 = 432 J.
  • Forgetting g – using kg as a force, or mixing g = 9.8 and 10. Weight = mg; use the g the question gives.
  • Not converting kJ → J, g → kg, minutes → seconds, km/h → m/s before substituting.
  • Thinking energy is “lost” in a fall. It changes form – PE becomes KE; friction turns some of it into heat, but the total stays the same.
  • Confusing power with energy or work. Power is the rate; more work does not always mean more power (Babu and Raju, Q14: both 30 W).
  • Treating work or energy as vectors. They are scalars – add them directly: 18 J + 15 J = 33 J (Q7), whatever the directions.
