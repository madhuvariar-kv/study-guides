Parallel Lines സമാന്തരവരകൾ

How a set of parallel lines cuts every line that crosses them into equal parts, or into parts in a fixed ratio; how that lets you divide any line, build triangles and rectangles with a given perimeter, and prove the midpoint theorem; and how the medians and altitudes of a triangle always meet at one point. An interactive, animated self-study guide: the basics from earlier classes, every idea of the chapter with real-life uses, animated solutions to every textbook question, a revision sheet, and a model test with a fully solved paper.

        The chapter in one picture

        📏 A stick across ruled paper is cut into equal parts

        Lay a stick across the lines of a ruled notebook so that it reaches from one line to the n-th line below. The lines cut it into n equal pieces, whatever the tilt. Equally spaced parallel lines cut every line into equal parts – that single fact drives the whole chapter.
