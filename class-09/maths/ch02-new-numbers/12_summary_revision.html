Key 1 · p. 20 ഒരു ഭിന്നസംഖ്യയുടെയും വർഗം 2 അല്ല.

No fraction has square 2. 1¼, 1⅓, 1½, … all miss: their squares are 1 9/16, 1 7/9, 2¼ – close to 2, never 2.

The diagonal of the unit square is not a fraction. The square on it has area 2 (four half-squares), so its side would have to square to 2.

Give the length a name: the side of the square of area 2 is written √2. Just as √4 = 2 and √(2¼) = 1½.

For any positive x, √x is the side of the square of area x. Sometimes a natural number or a fraction (√9 = 3, √(1 9/16) = 1¼); otherwise a decimal that never ends.

3. The fan of triangles and the perimeter facts

The fan (spiral) of right triangles

Each triangle has one side 1; the hypotenuse of each is a side of the next. The nth triangle has sides 1, √n, √(n + 1). Natural-number hypotenuses appear at the 3rd (√4 = 2), 8th (√9 = 3), 15th (√16 = 4) triangles.

4. Sum and square root · Decimal forms

√x + √y > √(x + y)

Squares of areas x and y corner to corner; the square on the third side of the triangle between them has area x + y. The triangle's sides are √x, √y and √(x + y), and two sides of a triangle together exceed the third. Example: 3 + 4 = 7 > 5 = √25.

5. The appendix proof in five lines അനുബന്ധം

  1. Suppose a fraction has square 2. Write it in lowest terms as x/y – x and y have no common factor.
  2. Then x²/y² = 2, so x² = 2y²: x² is even. An odd number has an odd square, so x is even.
  3. x and y have no common factor and x is even, so y is odd (otherwise 2 would divide both).
  4. Write x = 2z. Then 4z² = 2y², so y² = 2z²: y² is even, so y is even.
  5. y is odd AND y is even – impossible. So the supposition was wrong: no fraction has square 2. ∎ (The same idea with "multiple of 3" works for √3.)

6. Common mistakes

⚠️ Watch out for these
  • Writing √2 = 1.41. It is √2 ≈ 1.41. The decimal is a rounded value; 1.41² = 1.9881, not 2.
  • Thinking a longer decimal is exact. 1.41421 is closer, but 1.41421² = 1.9999899241 – still not 2. No decimal ever is.
  • Adding under the root. √2 + √3 is NOT √5: 1.414 + 1.732 = 3.146, while √5 ≈ 2.236. In fact √x + √y > √(x + y) always.
  • Rounding to the wrong place. "Correct to a millimetre" means 3 decimal places of a metre: 3.414 m, not 3.41 m.
  • Calling every square root a new number. √4 = 2, √(2¼) = 1½ and √(1 9/16) = 1¼ ARE fractions. Only when no fraction squares to the number is it new.
  • Confusing "square" with "double". The square on the diagonal has twice the area, but the diagonal is √2 ≈ 1.414 times the side, not 2 times.
  • Forgetting the half. The height of an equilateral triangle of side 2 uses the half-base 1: height² = 2² − 1² = 3, not 2² − 2².
