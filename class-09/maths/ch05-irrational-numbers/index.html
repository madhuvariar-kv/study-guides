        The chapter in one picture

        🐚 The square-root spiral: √2, √3, √4, … are real lengths

        Start with a right triangle with legs 1 and 1: the hypotenuse is √2. Add a new leg of length 1 at right angles to that hypotenuse and you get √3, then √4 = 2, √5, … Every one of these is an exact length you can draw, even though most of them can never be written as a fraction. This chapter teaches you to multiply and divide such numbers and to use them for areas.
