        The chapter in one picture

        ▦ A rectangle cut into four: (x + y)(u + v) = xu + xv + yu + yv

        Change the four numbers. The big rectangle is (x + y) wide and (u + v) tall, so its area is the product of the two sums. Cutting it along the dotted lines gives four smaller rectangles whose areas are the four products. That one picture is the whole chapter: everything else is this idea used cleverly.
