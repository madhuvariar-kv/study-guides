1.
A box has 80 beads, some black and some white. There are 12 more black beads than white beads. If the number of white beads is x, which equation gives x?
(a) x + 12 = 80(b) x + (x − 12) = 80(c) 2x + 12 = 80(d) 12x = 80
[1]

Step-by-step solution

  1. White beads = x, so black beads = x + 12.
  2. Together: x + (x + 12) = 80, that is 2x + 12 = 80.
  3. (Solving: 2x = 68, x = 34 white and 46 black beads; 34 + 46 = 80 ✓.)
✅ Answer: (c) 2x + 12 = 80

Marking: 1 mark for (c).

4.
The numbers 1 to 36 are written in rows of 6 (1–6 in the first row, 7–12 in the second, and so on). In any 2 × 2 square of these numbers, the products of the numbers at opposite corners always differ by
(a) 1(b) 5(c) 6(d) 7
[1]

Step-by-step solution

  1. A 2 × 2 square looks like x, x + 1 (top row) and x + 6, x + 7 (next row), since each row has 6 numbers.
  2. x(x + 7) = x² + 7x and (x + 1)(x + 6) = x² + 6x + x + 6 = x² + 7x + 6.
  3. The difference is always 6. (Example: 8, 9, 14, 15: 9 × 14 = 126 and 8 × 15 = 120.)
✅ Answer: (c) 6

Marking: 1 mark for (c).

6.
A triangle has sides 2 cm, 3 cm and 4 cm. Which of these triangles has the same angles as it?
(a) 4 cm, 6 cm, 7 cm(b) 3 cm, 4.5 cm, 6 cm(c) 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm(d) 4 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm
[1]

Step-by-step solution

  1. 3 = 1½ × 2, 4.5 = 1½ × 3 and 6 = 1½ × 4: all the sides change by the same scale 1½.

  3. In the others the scale is not the same for all sides: (a) 4/2 = 2 but 7/4 = 1¾; (c) 3/2 = 1½ but 4/3 = 1⅓; (d) 4/2 = 2 but 5/3 = 1⅔.
✅ Answer: (b) 3 cm, 4.5 cm, 6 cm

8.
Which of these statements about π is correct?
(a) π is exactly equal to 22/7.(b) π is the circumference of the circle of diameter 1, and it cannot be written as a fraction.(c) π is exactly equal to 3.14.(d) π is larger for larger circles.
[1]

Step-by-step solution

  1. The circumferences of all circles change in the same proportion as their diameters, so circumference = π × diameter, where π is the circumference of the circle of diameter 1. So π is the same for every circle.
  2. π cannot be written as a fraction; 22/7 and 3.14 are only approximations (π = 3.14159…).
✅ Answer: (b) π is the circumference of the circle of diameter 1, and it cannot be written as a fraction.

10.
True or false? Give a reason:   “For every real number x, |x| = x.”
[1]

Step-by-step solution

  1. |x| (കേവലമൂല്യം) is x when x is positive or 0, but it is −x when x is negative.
  2. Example: x = −3 gives |−3| = 3, which is not −3. So the statement is false (it is true only for x ≥ 0).
✅ Answer: False. For example |−3| = 3, which is not −3.

11.
A pen and a notebook together cost ₹70. The notebook costs ₹4 more than twice the price of the pen. Taking the price of the pen as ₹x, form an equation and find the price of each.
[2]

Step-by-step solution

  2. Together: x + (2x + 4) = 70, so 3x + 4 = 70 (സമവാക്യം).
  3. 3x = 66, so x = 22. The pen costs ₹22.
  4. Notebook = 2 × 22 + 4 = ₹48. Check: 22 + 48 = 70 ✓.
  5. (Mentally: remove the extra ₹4; the remaining ₹66 is the price of three pens.)
✅ Answer: Pen ₹22, notebook ₹48

13.
Two natural numbers each leave remainder 2 on division by 3. Prove that their product leaves remainder 1 on division by 3. Check with one example.
[2]

Step-by-step solution

  1. Numbers leaving remainder 2 on division by 3 have the form 3m + 2 and 3n + 2 (m, n = 0, 1, 2, …).
  2. (3m + 2)(3n + 2) = 9mn + 6m + 6n + 4 (multiply each number of the first sum by each number of the second).
  3. = 9mn + 6m + 6n + 3 + 1 = 3(3mn + 2m + 2n + 1) + 1.
  4. This is one more than a multiple of 3, so the remainder is 1.
  5. Example: 5 × 8 = 40 = 3 × 13 + 1 ✓.
✅ Answer: (3m + 2)(3n + 2) = 3(3mn + 2m + 2n + 1) + 1, which leaves remainder 1.

Marking: Correct general forms ½; expansion ½; writing as 3( … ) + 1 with the conclusion ½; example ½.

19.
Ammu’s mother is 26 years older than Ammu. After 6 years, the mother’s age will be 3 times Ammu’s age then. Taking Ammu’s present age as x years: (a) write the ages of both after 6 years in terms of x; (b) form an equation and solve it; (c) find their present ages and check.
[3]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) Now: Ammu x, mother x + 26. After 6 years: Ammu x + 6, mother x + 32.
  2. (b) Mother then = 3 × Ammu then: x + 32 = 3(x + 6) = 3x + 18.
  3. 32 − 18 = 3x − x, so 2x = 14 and x = 7.
  4. (c) Ammu is 7 years old and her mother is 7 + 26 = 33 years old.
  5. Check: after 6 years they are 13 and 39, and 39 = 3 × 13 ✓.
  6. (Mental way: the difference of their ages is always 26. When the mother is 3 times Ammu’s age, the difference is 2 times Ammu’s age, so Ammu will be 13 then, and is 7 now.)
✅ Answer: Ammu 7 years, mother 33 years

Marking: (a) 1 mark; (b) equation 1, solving ½; (c) ages and check ½.

20.
In the calendar a 3 × 3 square of dates is marked. (a) Find the products of the dates at opposite corners of the marked square, 4 × 20 and 6 × 18, and their difference. (b) Prove that for every 3 × 3 square of dates in a calendar this difference is the same. (c) What is the difference for a 4 × 4 square? Give the reason.
[3]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) 4 × 20 = 80 and 6 × 18 = 108; difference 108 − 80 = 28.
  2. (b) A row has 7 days, so if the top-left date is x, the corners are x, x + 2 (top) and x + 14, x + 16 (bottom).
  3. x(x + 16) = x² + 16x.
  4. (x + 2)(x + 14) = x² + 14x + 2x + 28 = x² + 16x + 28.
  5. The difference is always 28, whatever x is.
  6. (c) Corners of a 4 × 4 square: x, x + 3, x + 21, x + 24. (x + 3)(x + 21) = x² + 24x + 63 and x(x + 24) = x² + 24x, so the difference is 63. (Check: 4 × 22 − 1 × 25 = 88 − 25 = 63.)
✅ Answer: (a) 80 and 108, difference 28   (b) always 28   (c) 63

Marking: (a) ½; (b) corners ½, both products and the conclusion 1; (c) 1.

21.
(a) Show that (√7 + √5)(√7 − √5) = 2.   (b) Using this, write 2√7 − √5 with no square root in the denominator.   (c) Taking √7 ≈ 2.646 and √5 ≈ 2.236, find its value correct to two decimal places.
[3]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) The identity (x + y)(x − y) = x² − y² holds for irrational numbers too: (√7 + √5)(√7 − √5) = (√7)² − (√5)² = 7 − 5 = 2.
  2. (b) Multiply the numerator and the denominator by √7 + √5: 2√7 − √5 = 2(√7 + √5)(√7 − √5)(√7 + √5) = 2(√7 + √5)2 = √7 + √5.
  3. (c) √7 + √5 ≈ 2.646 + 2.236 = 4.882 ≈ 4.88. (Adding is much easier than dividing 2 by 0.41.)
✅ Answer: (a) 7 − 5 = 2   (b) √7 + √5   (c) ≈ 4.88

Marking: 1 mark for each part.

22.
In the figure, ΔABC has sides 4 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm and angles 83°, 56°, 41° (to the nearest degree). ΔPQR has sides PR = 6 cm, QR = 7.5 cm and PQ = 9 cm. (a) Show that the sides of the two triangles are in the same ratio. (b) Find ∠P, ∠Q and ∠R, giving the reason. (c) How many times the area of ΔABC is the area of ΔPQR?
[3]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) Take the sides in order of size: 4, 5, 6 and 6, 7.5, 9. 6/4 = 1½, 7.5/5 = 1½, 9/6 = 1½. All the sides change by the same scale 1½.
  2. (b) If all the sides change by the same scale, the triangles have the same angles (സദൃശം); equal angles are opposite matching sides.
  3. Opposite the smallest sides: ∠C (opposite AB = 4) and ∠Q (opposite PR = 6), so ∠Q = 41°.
  4. Opposite the middle sides: ∠B (opposite CA = 5) and ∠P (opposite QR = 7.5), so ∠P = 56°.
  5. Opposite the largest sides: ∠A (opposite BC = 6) and ∠R (opposite PQ = 9), so ∠R = 83°. (56 + 41 + 83 = 180 ✓)
  6. (c) The area changes by the square of the scale: (1½)² = 2¼ times.
✅ Answer: (a) each side × 1½   (b) ∠P = 56°, ∠Q = 41°, ∠R = 83°   (c) 2¼ times

Marking: (a) 1 mark; (b) reason ½, the three angles 1 (deduct ½ for each wrong angle); (c) ½.

23.
(a) Using the meaning of subtraction (“how much must be added?”), explain why 4 − (−6) = 10. (b) In a building the basement floors are numbered −1, −2, −3, …. A lift goes from floor −3 up to floor 7 and then down to floor −2. Write the change of floor for each trip as (final floor − starting floor). (c) Find the change from floor −3 to floor −2 directly and show that it is the sum of the two changes in (b).
[3]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) 4 − (−6) asks: how much must be added to −6 to get 4?
  2. From −6, adding 6 reaches 0; adding 4 more reaches 4. So we add 6 + 4 = 10, and 4 − (−6) = 10.
  3. (b) First trip: 7 − (−3) = 7 + 3 = 10 (10 floors up).
  4. Second trip: −2 − 7 = −(2 + 7) = −9 (9 floors down).
  5. (c) Directly: −2 − (−3) = −2 + 3 = 1 (1 floor up). And 10 + (−9) = 10 − 9 = 1 ✓.
  6. This always happens: (z − y) + (y − x) = z − x; the total change is final − start.
✅ Answer: (a) add 6 to reach 0, then 4 more: 10   (b) 10 and −9   (c) 1 = 10 + (−9)

Marking: (a) 1 mark; (b) ½ each; (c) 1 mark.

24.
A window has the shape shown: a rectangle 1.4 m wide and 1.5 m high with a semicircle on top. (a) Find the length of the semicircular arc. (b) Find the perimeter of the window. (c) Another window of the same shape is twice as wide and twice as high. What is its perimeter? (Take π = 22/7.)
[3]

  1. (a) The semicircle has diameter 1.4 m. Its arc is half the circumference: ½ × π × 1.4 = 0.7π m.
  2. 0.7π = 0.7 × 22/7 = 0.1 × 22 = 2.2 m.
  3. (b) The boundary is the arc, the two vertical sides and the bottom side (the dashed line is inside the window): 2.2 + 1.5 + 1.5 + 1.4 = 6.6 m.

✅ Answer: (a) 2.2 m   (b) 6.6 m   (c) 13.2 m

26.
On a map, a line AB of length 10 cm shows a straight stretch of a canal between two boat jetties A and B. A new jetty P is to be built on AB so that AP : PB = 3 : 4. (a) Calculate the lengths AP and PB on the map. (b) Draw AB = 10 cm and mark P using a ruler, compass and set squares, without measuring AP. Write the steps of construction. (c) Explain why your point P divides AB in the ratio 3 : 4.
[4]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) 3 + 4 = 7 parts. AP = 10 × 3/7 = 30/7 = 4 2/7 ≈ 4.29 cm; PB = 10 × 4/7 = 40/7 = 5 5/7 ≈ 5.71 cm. These are not easy to measure with a ruler, so we construct P.
  2. (b) Step 1: Draw AB = 10 cm.
  3. Step 2: From A draw a ray AX making an acute angle with AB.
  4. Step 3: With the compass mark 7 equal steps along AX from A (any convenient length, e.g. 1 cm): A₁, A₂, …, A₇.
  5. Step 4: Join A₇ to B.
  6. Step 5: Through A₃ draw a line parallel to A₇B (slide a set square along a ruler). It meets AB at P.
  8. (c) In ΔAA₇B the line A₃P is parallel to the side A₇B, so it cuts the other two sides in the same ratio (അംശബന്ധം): AP : PB = AA₃ : A₃A₇ = 3 : 4 (three equal steps and four equal steps).
✅ Answer: (a) AP = 30/7 ≈ 4.29 cm, PB = 40/7 ≈ 5.71 cm   (b) see the construction   (c) A₃P ∥ A₇B cuts AB and AX in the same ratio

Marking: (a) 1 mark; (b) construction with steps 2 marks (ray and equal steps ½, joining A₇B ½, parallel through A₃ 1); (c) 1 mark.

27.
A KSRTC bus runs on a straight east–west road. The road is drawn as a number line with the bus stand O at 0; distances are in km, east is positive. The stops are A (−6), J (−1), O (0), C (2) and B (9). (a) Find the distance between A and B using absolute value. (b) Raju’s house is on the road, 4 km from C. Write this as an equation with absolute value and find the possible positions of his house. (c) A fuel station is to be built less than 3 km from the junction J. Write this condition with absolute value, find the possible positions and show them on the number line. (d) Which of the stops are more than 5 km from C?
[4]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) The distance between x and y on the number line is |x − y|: |−6 − 9| = |−15| = 15 km (bigger minus smaller: 9 − (−6) = 15).
  2. (b) Position x with distance 4 from 2: |x − 2| = 4.
  3. 4 km east of C: x = 2 + 4 = 6; 4 km west of C: x = 2 − 4 = −2.
  4. (c) Distance from J: |x − (−1)| = |x + 1|. Condition: |x + 1| < 3.
  5. So x lies within 3 km on either side of −1: from −1 − 3 = −4 to −1 + 3 = 2, that is −4 < x < 2.
  7. (d) Distances from C (2): A: |−6 − 2| = 8, J: |−1 − 2| = 3, O: |0 − 2| = 2, B: |9 − 2| = 7.
  8. More than 5 km: A and B. (In algebra: |x − 2| > 5 means x < −3 or x > 7.)
✅ Answer: (a) 15 km   (b) |x − 2| = 4: 6 or −2   (c) |x + 1| < 3: −4 < x < 2   (d) A and B

28.
A rectangular paddy field is 10 m longer than it is wide. Its width is x m. A bund 1 m wide is made all round it, outside the field. (a) Write the area a(x) of the field as a polynomial. What is its degree? Write its coefficients. (b) Write the area b(x) of the bund as a polynomial and give its degree. (c) Is the length of the diagonal of the field a polynomial in x? Is the perimeter of the field a polynomial? Give reasons. (d) Find a(20) and b(20).
[4]

Step-by-step solution

  1. (a) a(x) = x(x + 10) = x² + 10x. The highest power is x², so the degree is 2. Coefficients: of x² it is 1, of x it is 10, and the constant term is 0.
  2. (b) With the bund, the outer rectangle is (x + 2) m by (x + 12) m. Its area = (x + 2)(x + 12) = x² + 12x + 2x + 24 = x² + 14x + 24.
  3. b(x) = (x² + 14x + 24) − (x² + 10x) = 4x + 24; a first degree polynomial (degree 1).
  4. (c) Diagonal = √x² + (x + 10)² = √2x² + 20x + 100: it takes the square root of an expression with the changing number x, so it is not a polynomial (ബഹുപദം).
  5. Perimeter = 2(x + x + 10) = 4x + 20: only multiplying x by a fixed number and adding a fixed number, so it is a polynomial (degree 1).
  6. (d) a(20) = 20² + 10 × 20 = 400 + 200 = 600 sq m (check: 20 × 30 = 600).
  7. b(20) = 4 × 20 + 24 = 104 sq m (check: 22 × 32 − 600 = 704 − 600 = 104).
✅ Answer: (a) x² + 10x, degree 2   (b) 4x + 24, degree 1   (c) diagonal: no; perimeter: yes   (d) 600 sq m and 104 sq m

Marking: (a) polynomial ½, degree and coefficients ½; (b) 1 mark; (c) ½ + ½; (d) ½ + ½.
