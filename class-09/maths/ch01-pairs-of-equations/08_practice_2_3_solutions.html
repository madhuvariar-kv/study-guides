Practice Sets 2 & 3 – Animated Solutions ചിന്തിക്കാം

Five questions from the "think" boxes after the pens-and-pencils and the water-vessels examples. In every one, two facts become two equations; then we make one letter's coefficients equal, subtract (or add), and that letter disappears. Try each in your notebook first – the hint is there if you are stuck – then press Next ▶ to watch the solution grow.

📖 Tools you will use (pages 2, 3 and 7 of this guide)
  • Same coefficient? Subtract the equations: 2x + 5y − (2x + 3y) = 2y.
  • Different coefficients? Multiply one or both equations first, so that one letter has the same coefficient in both.
  • Swapped coefficients (2 and 3, 3 and 2)? Adding the equations gives a multiple of x + y; subtracting gives x − y.
  • Always check by putting the answers back into both facts.

Practice set 2 · Question 1 – pens and pencils

Q1Four pens and three pencils cost ₹66. Seven pens and three pencils cost ₹111. What is the price of a pen? And of a pencil?
(നാല് പേനയും, മൂന്ന് പെൻസിലും കൂടി 66 രൂപയാണ് വില. ഏഴ് പേനയും, മൂന്ന് പെൻസിലും കൂടി 111 രൂപ. ഒരു പേനയുടെ വില എത്രയാണ്? പെൻസിലിന്റെ വിലയോ?)

Both bills have the same 3 pencils. So what does the extra ₹45 pay for?

🎬 Solution animation
Answer: pen x, pencil y: 4x + 3y = 66 and 7x + 3y = 111. Subtracting, 3x = 45, so a pen costs ₹15. Then 3y = 66 − 60 = 6, so a pencil costs ₹2. Check: 105 + 6 = 111 ✓
🌍 Why this matters

When two bills share exactly the same items except one kind, the difference of the bills is the price of the extra items only. Shopkeepers check bills this way every day.

Practice set 2 · Question 2 – the stretched rectangle

Q2The perimeter of a rectangle is 26 cm. When its length is doubled and its breadth tripled, the new rectangle has perimeter 62 cm. Find the length and breadth of the original rectangle.

Half the perimeter is length + breadth. Write l + b = 13 and 2l + 3b = 31. Doubling the first equation makes the l's match.

🎬 Solution animation
Answer: l + b = 13 and 2l + 3b = 31. Doubling the first: 2l + 2b = 26. Subtracting: b = 31 − 26 = 5. So breadth 5 cm, length 8 cm. Check: new sides 16 and 15, perimeter 2 × 31 = 62 ✓
💡 Note

Never forget to halve the perimeter: a rectangle's perimeter is 2(l + b), so "perimeter 26" means l + b = 13.

Practice set 3 · Question 1 – oranges and apples

Q12 kg of oranges and 3 kg of apples cost ₹520; 3 kg of oranges and 2 kg of apples cost ₹480. What is the price of 1 kg of oranges? Of apples?
(രണ്ടു കിലോഗ്രാം മധുരനാരങ്ങയും മൂന്നു കിലോഗ്രാം ആപ്പിളും കൂടി 520 രൂപ; മൂന്നു കിലോഗ്രാം മധുരനാരങ്ങയും രണ്ടു കിലോഗ്രാം ആപ്പിളും കൂടി 480 രൂപ. ഒരു കിലോഗ്രാം മധുരനാരങ്ങയുടെ വില എത്രയാണ്? ആപ്പിളിന്റെയോ?)

The numbers 2 and 3 are swapped between the two bills. Put both bills together: how many kg of each fruit is that?

🎬 Solution animation – the add-and-subtract trick
Answer: 2x + 3y = 520, 3x + 2y = 480 (oranges x, apples y). Adding: 5x + 5y = 1000, so x + y = 200. Subtracting: y − x = 40. Then oranges ₹80 per kg, apples ₹120 per kg. Check: 160 + 360 = 520 ✓, 240 + 240 = 480 ✓
🌍 Why this matters

The usual method (multiply the first by 3 and the second by 2, then subtract) also works. But when the coefficients are swapped, adding and subtracting is much faster – look for it!

Practice set 3 · Question 2 – one wire, two shapes

Q2A wire 1 metre long is cut into two pieces. One piece is bent into a square and the other into an equilateral triangle. Three times the side of the square plus twice the side of the triangle is 71 cm. At what length was the wire cut?

Work in centimetres: 1 m = 100 cm. The square uses 4 sides and the triangle 3 sides, so 4s + 3t = 100. The other fact is 3s + 2t = 71.

🎬 Solution animation
Answer: 4s + 3t = 100 and 3s + 2t = 71. Multiply the first by 2 and the second by 3: 8s + 6t = 200 and 9s + 6t = 213. Subtracting: s = 13, then 3t = 100 − 52 = 48, t = 16. The square piece is 4 × 13 = 52 cm and the triangle piece 3 × 16 = 48 cm: cut the wire at 52 cm. Check: 39 + 32 = 71 ✓
💡 Note

The question asks where to cut, not the sides – so the last step turns the sides back into the lengths of the two pieces.

Practice set 3 · Question 3 – Rahim and Ramu

Q3Four years ago Rahim's age was three times Ramu's age. Two years from now it will be twice. What are their ages now?

Let their ages now be R and m. Four years ago they were R − 4 and m − 4; in two years R + 2 and m + 2.

🎬 Solution animation – a timeline
Answer: R − 4 = 3(m − 4) gives R = 3m − 8; R + 2 = 2(m + 2) gives R = 2m + 2. So 3m − 8 = 2m + 2 and m = 10, R = 22. Rahim is 22, Ramu is 10. Check: 18 = 3 × 6 ✓, 24 = 2 × 12 ✓
🌍 Why this matters

In every age puzzle the difference of ages never changes (here 12 years). It is a handy check on your answer.

🧭 Summary
  • Same items in both bills except one kind ⇒ subtract the bills.
  • Perimeter facts: halve first (l + b = half the perimeter), then match coefficients.
  • Swapped coefficients ⇒ add (gives x + y) and subtract (gives x − y).
  • Ages: write the past and future ages with the same letters, then solve.
