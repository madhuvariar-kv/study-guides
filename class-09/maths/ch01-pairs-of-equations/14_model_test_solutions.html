📖 How marks are usually given

1-mark questions: the correct answer only. Longer questions: about one mark for writing the equations correctly (say what x and y stand for), the rest for the working and the final answer. A correct method with a small arithmetic slip still earns most of the marks – so always write the equations and every step.

2.
Two numbers have sum 30 and difference 6. The larger number is .
(1)
Answer: 18

Larger = (sum + difference) ÷ 2 = (30 + 6) ÷ 2 = 18 (the smaller is 12).

3.
If 2x + 3y = 12 and 2x + y = 8, then 2y = .
(1)
Answer: 4

Subtract: (2x + 3y) − (2x + y) = 2y = 12 − 8 = 4. (So y = 2 and x = 3.)

4.
Which pair of equations has the solution x = 2, y = 3?
(a) x + y = 5, x − y = −1(b) x + y = 6, x − y = 1(c) 2x + y = 6, x + y = 5(d) x − y = 1, x + y = 5
(1)
Answer: (a)

2 + 3 = 5 and 2 − 3 = −1. In (b) 2 + 3 ≠ 6; in (c) 2 × 2 + 3 = 7 ≠ 6; in (d) 2 − 3 = −1 ≠ 1. A solution must satisfy both equations.

SECTION B5 × 2 = 10 marks
9.
The sum of two numbers is 40 and their difference is 10. Find the numbers.
(2)
🎬 Solution
Answer: 25 and 15

x + y = 40, x − y = 10. Adding: 2x = 50 ⇒ x = 25. Subtracting: 2y = 30 ⇒ y = 15. Check: 25 + 15 = 40, 25 − 15 = 10.

Marks: 1 for the two equations (or the bar-model reasoning), 1 for both numbers.

Review: Page 9 · Sum and difference

10.
A table and a chair together cost ₹5000. A table and 3 chairs cost ₹7000. Find the price of each.
(2)
🎬 Solution
Answer: chair ₹1000, table ₹4000

Chair x, table y: x + y = 5000, 3x + y = 7000. Subtract: 2x = 2000 ⇒ x = 1000; y = 5000 − 1000 = 4000.

Marks: 1 for the equations or "2 extra chairs cost ₹2000", 1 for both prices.

11.
Solve: 3x + 2y = 18, x + 2y = 10.
(2)
🎬 Solution
Answer: x = 4, y = 3

The y-coefficients are equal: subtract, (3x + 2y) − (x + 2y) = 2x = 8 ⇒ x = 4. Then 4 + 2y = 10 ⇒ y = 3. Check: 12 + 6 = 18 ✓.

Marks: 1 for eliminating y (2x = 8), 1 for x = 4, y = 3.

Review: Page 7 · Elimination

12.
Adding 2 to the numerator of a fraction and simplifying gives ½. Adding 1 to its denominator instead gives ⅓. Find the fraction.
(2)
🎬 Solution
Answer: 5/14

Numerator x, denominator y. (x + 2)/y = ½ ⇒ y = 2(x + 2) = 2x + 4. x/(y + 1) = ⅓ ⇒ y + 1 = 3x. Substitute: 2x + 5 = 3x ⇒ x = 5, y = 14. Check: 7/14 = ½ ✓, 5/15 = ⅓ ✓.

Marks: 1 for the two equations y = 2(x + 2), y + 1 = 3x; 1 for 5/14.

13.
The perimeter of a rectangle is 30 cm and its length is 3 cm more than its breadth. Find its length and breadth.
(2)
🎬 Solution
Answer: breadth 6 cm, length 9 cm

Perimeter 30 ⇒ length + breadth = 15. With l − b = 3: 2l = 18 ⇒ l = 9, b = 6. Check: 2(9 + 6) = 30 ✓.

Marks: 1 for l + b = 15 and l − b = 3 (or b + (b + 3) = 15), 1 for the sides.

SECTION C4 × 3 = 12 marks
14.
2 kg of apples and 3 kg of oranges cost ₹420. 3 kg of apples and 2 kg of oranges cost ₹380. Find the price of 1 kg of each.
(3)
🎬 Solution
Answer: apples ₹60/kg, oranges ₹100/kg

Apple x, orange y: 2x + 3y = 420, 3x + 2y = 380. Add: 5x + 5y = 800 ⇒ x + y = 160. Subtract: y − x = 40. So y = 100, x = 60. Check: 120 + 300 = 420 ✓, 180 + 200 = 380 ✓. (Making coefficients equal also works: × 3 and × 2.)

Marks: 1 for the equations, 1 for x + y = 160 and y − x = 40 (or an elimination step), 1 for both prices.

15.
Solve by making the coefficients of one letter equal: 4x + 3y = 24, 3x − 2y = 1.
(3)
🎬 Solution
Answer: x = 3, y = 4

(1) × 2: 8x + 6y = 48. (2) × 3: 9x − 6y = 3. The y-terms are opposite, so add: 17x = 51 ⇒ x = 3. Then 12 + 3y = 24 ⇒ y = 4. Check (2): 9 − 8 = 1 ✓.

Marks: 1 for multiplying correctly, 1 for 17x = 51 (or the matching step for x), 1 for x = 3, y = 4.

Review: Page 7 · Making coefficients equal

16.
The digits of a two-digit number add up to 9. Reversing the digits gives a number 27 more than the original. Find the number.
(3)
🎬 Solution
Answer: 36

Number 10a + b, reversed 10b + a. a + b = 9 and (10b + a) − (10a + b) = 9b − 9a = 27 ⇒ b − a = 3. Sum and difference: b = 6, a = 3. Check: 63 − 36 = 27 ✓.

Marks: 1 for a + b = 9, 1 for 9(b − a) = 27, 1 for 36.

17.
₹50000 is invested in two schemes, one at 8% and the other at 6% yearly interest. The total interest after one year is ₹3500. How much was invested in each scheme?
(3)
🎬 Solution
Answer: ₹25000 in each

x at 8%, y at 6%: x + y = 50000, 0.08x + 0.06y = 3500. Subtract 0.06 × (first): 0.02x = 3500 − 3000 = 500 ⇒ x = 25000, y = 25000. Check: 2000 + 1500 = 3500 ✓.

Marks: 1 for the equations, 1 for 0.02x = 500, 1 for both amounts.

SECTION D2 × 5 = 10 marks
18.
Five years ago a father was 3 times as old as his son. Five years from now he will be twice as old as his son.
  1. Write two equations for their present ages.
  2. Solve them.
  3. Check your answer with both facts.
  4. Explain why the difference of their ages is the same at every time.
(5)
🎬 Solution
Answer: son 15 years, father 35 years

(i) Father F, son S now: F − 5 = 3(S − 5) and F + 5 = 2(S + 5), that is F = 3S − 10 and F = 2S + 5.

(ii) 3S − 10 = 2S + 5 ⇒ S = 15, F = 35.

(iii) Five years ago: 30 and 10, and 30 = 3 × 10 ✓. In five years: 40 and 20, and 40 = 2 × 20 ✓.

(iv) Every year both become one year older, so the gap F − S = 20 never changes.

Marks: 2 for the two equations, 1 for S = 15, F = 35, 1 for the check, 1 for the explanation.

19.
The speed of an object after t seconds is u + at metres per second. After 2 seconds its speed is 11 m/s; after 6 seconds it is 23 m/s.
  1. Write two equations in u and a.
  2. Find u and a.
  3. Find its speed after 10 seconds.
  4. The distance travelled in t seconds is ut + ½at². Find the distance travelled in the first 4 seconds.
  5. Explain why the pair x + y = 5, 2x + 2y = 12 has no solution.
(5)
🎬 Solution
Answer: u = 5 m/s, a = 3 m/s per second; 35 m/s; 44 m

(i) u + 2a = 11 and u + 6a = 23.

(ii) Subtract: 4a = 12 ⇒ a = 3; u = 11 − 6 = 5.

(iii) Speed after 10 s = 5 + 3 × 10 = 35 m/s.

(iv) Distance = 5 × 4 + ½ × 3 × 16 = 20 + 24 = 44 m.

(v) Doubling the first equation gives 2x + 2y = 10, but the second says 2x + 2y = 12. The same quantity cannot be both 10 and 12, so the facts contradict each other and no x, y satisfy both.

Marks: 1 for each part.
