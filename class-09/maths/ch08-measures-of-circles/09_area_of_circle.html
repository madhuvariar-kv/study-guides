Area of a Circle പരപ്പളവ്

We trapped the circumference between polygons. The same trick finds the area: polygons inside a circle fill it more and more completely as their sides increase, and the area of each polygon is easy to compute. The result is short and famous: area = ½ × circumference × radius = πr².

📖 What you already know
  • Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height.
  • Circumference c = 2πr (page 7 of this guide).
  • Regular polygons in a circle come closer and closer to the circle as the number of sides grows (page 5).

⭐ Key box (textbook page 160)

വൃത്തത്തിന്റെ പരപ്പളവ്, അതിന്റെ ചുറ്റളവിന്റെയും ആരത്തിന്റെയും ഗുണനഫലത്തിന്റെ പകുതിയാണ്

The area of a circle is half the product of its circumference and its radius: a = ½cr.

Since c = 2πr: a = ½ × 2πr × r = πr².

വൃത്തത്തിന്റെ പരപ്പളവ്, ആരവർഗത്തിന്റെ π മടങ്ങാണ് – the area of a circle is π times the square of its radius. Example: radius 5 cm ⇒ area 25π sq cm ≈ 78.54 sq cm.

4. Another way to see it: slices into a rectangle

another way to see it Cut the circle into equal slices (sectors) and lay them alternately point-up and point-down. The more slices, the more the shape looks like a rectangle whose length is half the circumference and whose height is the radius.

🍕 Rearrange the slices

Length ≈ half the circumference = πr, height ≈ r, so area ≈ πr × r = πr² – the same answer.

5. Rings വൃത്തവലയം

🎬 Animation: the ring between radii 2.5 cm and 2 cm
🎚️ Any ring has a circle twin

Ring area = πR² − πr² = π(R² − r²). In a right triangle with hypotenuse R and one side r, the third side t has t² = R² − r², so the circle of radius t has exactly the ring's area.

💻 Try in GeoGebra (textbook side boxes)

Draw a circle of radius √(10/π) with Circle: Centre & Radius (type sqrt(10/pi)) and measure it with the Area tool: the area is 10. Then try to draw a circle of area 25 (radius √(25/π) ≈ 2.82).

🌍 Real life: pizza sizes

A 12-inch pizza (radius 6) has area 36π; an 8-inch pizza (radius 4) has 16π. One 12-inch pizza is 2¼ times one 8-inch pizza, and more than two 8-inch pizzas together (32π). Area grows with the square of the radius.

🧭 Summary
  • A regular polygon in a circle has area ½ × perimeter × h (h = perpendicular from the centre to a side).
  • As the sides increase, perimeters approach c and h approaches r, so the polygon areas approach ½cr.
  • Area of a circle = ½cr = πr². Radius 5 ⇒ 25π.
  • Ring between radii R and r: π(R² − r²); it equals a circle whose radius is the third side of a right triangle with hypotenuse R and side r.
