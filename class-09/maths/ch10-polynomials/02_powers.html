Prerequisite 2 of 4Classes 7–8: powers

Squares, Cubes and Powers കൃതികൾ

Polynomials are built from powers (കൃതി) of a number: x, x², x³ and so on. Here we see what x² and x³ really mean – the area of a square and the volume of a cube – how place value is secretly made of powers of 10, and what happens when we square or cube negative numbers and fractions.

📖 What you need
  • Multiplication of whole numbers, fractions and negative numbers (a negative times a negative is positive).
  • Letters for numbers (page 1).

1. x² – the square of a number

A square tile of side 3 cm can be filled with 3 rows of 3 small 1 cm squares: 3 × 3 = 9 squares. So we write 3 × 3 as 3² (“3 squared”), the square (വർഗം) of 3. For any number x, x² = x × x is the area of a square of side x.

🎚️ Build a square
⚠️ The most common mistake

x² is x × x, not 2 × x. For x = 5: x² = 25 but 2x = 10. They agree only when x = 2 (and x = 0).

2. x³ – the cube of a number

A cube of side 2 cm is built from 2 layers, each 2 × 2: so it holds 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 small unit cubes. We write 2³ (“2 cubed”). For any x, x³ = x × x × x is the volume of a cube of side x.

3. Higher powers, and the power of 10

We can keep multiplying: x⁴ = x × x × x × x, x⁵ = five x's multiplied, and so on. The small raised number counts how many times x is multiplied – it is the exponent (കൃത്യങ്കം). x itself is x¹. In this chapter the word കൃത്യങ്കം will also mean the degree of a polynomial – the biggest exponent in it.

🎚️ Powers of a number

Powers grow very fast: 2¹⁰ = 1024. That is why x³ soon becomes much bigger than x² and x.

Our ordinary way of writing numbers is built from powers of 10. The digits of 4237 say “4 thousands, 2 hundreds, 3 tens and 7 ones”:

🎬 Animation: 4237 = 4 × 10³ + 2 × 10² + 3 × 10 + 7
🌍 Real life

A computer stores numbers using powers of 2 instead of 10: the binary number 1011 means 1 × 2³ + 0 × 2² + 1 × 2 + 1 = 8 + 0 + 2 + 1 = 11. Data sizes are powers of 2 too: 2¹⁰ = 1024 bytes in a kilobyte.

4. Powers of negative numbers and fractions

Use the sign rule: negative × negative = positive. So an even number of negative factors gives a positive answer, an odd number gives a negative one.

🎬 Animation: signs of powers
🧪 Any base, any exponent

Fractions: (½)² = ½ × ½ = ¼ – squaring a number smaller than 1 makes it smaller. For a mixed number like 1½, the square is not 1¼! The picture shows why: a square of side 1½ is made of a 1 × 1 square, two ½ × 1 strips and a ½ × ½ corner.

🎬 Animation: (1½)² = 2¼
⭐ Key facts
  • x² = x × x (area of a square), x³ = x × x × x (volume of a cube), xⁿ = n x's multiplied.
  • (−1)² = 1, (−1)³ = −1, (−2)² = 4, (−2)³ = −8: even powers of a negative number are positive, odd powers negative.
  • (½)² = ¼, (1½)² = 2¼, 0² = 0, 1² = 1³ = 1.
